# WP-35 handoff: /data/, "The long game, in data" (#84)

## Routes
`/data/` (HubLayout) and four report pages (MarketingLayout): `/data/survival/`, `/data/formation/`, `/data/who-starts/`, `/data/years-to-milestones/`. Rows sit inside the WP-35 block of `src/data/routes.ts`. CTAs: "Start a readiness review" (/apply/), "See how long it really takes" (/why-it-takes-time/).

## Datasets (retrieved 2026-10-15; registry in `src/data/datasets/sources.ts`)
- BLS Business Employment Dynamics, Table 7 (total private, Information, Professional/Scientific/Technical), data to March 2025.
- Census Business Formation Statistics monthly CSV (applications by sector 2005-2025, 4- and 8-quarter formations) and its definitions page.
- Kauffman 2025 National Report (rate of new entrepreneurs by age) and "Who Is the Entrepreneur" 2021 (age shares).
- Azoulay, Jones, Kim, Miranda, NBER WP 24489 (Table 5, founder age).
- Carta State of Private Markets Q1 2026: single published figures only (round gaps, median age by round, dilution); never re-hosted.
- CB Insights startup failure report (22 months from last raise to shutdown), SBA Advocacy FAQ 2026, GEM US 2019/2020 (definition of "established").
Each dataset records source id, retrieval date and transformation in its file; `.astro-magazine/sources/wp-35.json` is the ledger (all 12 in fetches since the WP-32b host approval). `src/data/claims.ts` is outside this write set, so the ledger lives in that JSON.

## Components for reuse (WP-37 and others), `src/components/visuals/charts/`
All take `FigureMeta` (`id`, `title`, `description`, `sources`, `retrieved`, optional `derived`, `caption`), render pure SVG at build time with zero client JS, include a data table in `<details>`, SourceNote with retrieval date, forced-colors and reduced-motion handling, and an optional `animation-timeline: view()` reveal behind `@supports`.
StackedArea, SlopeChart, WaffleChart (100 or 1000 squares), SmallMultiples, Ridgeline, RangeDotPlot, BeeswarmStrip, ScatterPlot, GanttTimeline, plus ChartFigure (shell), DataStage (band section), DataSources (sources list with `#source-<id>` anchors) and `dataviz.ts` helpers. Ready-made props for every chart are in `src/data/datasets/figures.ts`; copy in `copy.ts`. WP-37 can import e.g. `survivalWaffle` or `milestoneGantt` directly.

## Decisions

- No `CtaBand`: the CTA map resolves `/investors/` to primary `investor-interest` and secondary `brief:early-stage-diligence`, and `slot="after"` would have suppressed the automatic end band. The `/investors/interest/` prefix has no CTA, as the map says.
- `PortfolioShowcase` reads `getCollection('portfolio')` directly, because `getPublished` excludes `portfolio`. This is a recorded deviation from `src/lib/content.ts` (a frozen file).
- The add-on is named only where the verbatim `INTRODUCTIONS_DISCLAIMER` or `InvestorIntroductionsOffer` / `IntroductionsDisclaimer` renders with it. `copy.ts` never names it; the one literal copy of the disclaimer outside the shared block is the FAQ answer, and a test asserts it equals the constant.
- Every page keeps "WE makes no promise of funding." The hub keeps "Not an offer of securities" and "The fee does not depend on whether any investment closes." No page makes a regulatory statement, so nothing is fetched.
- No response-time promise (DP-5) and no count, amount or social proof.

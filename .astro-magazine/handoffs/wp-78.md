# WP-78 handoff

FAQ at scale (issue #58). Branch `wp/78`, integration branch `demo`.

## What shipped

- **`src/data/faq.yaml`: 18 to 64 entries.** The 18 originals are untouched (byte for byte, pinned by `tests/unit/wp-60/reference-data.test.ts`) and stay general (no `audience`). WP-78 appends 46 entries after `not-for`.

  | Audience | Entries | Of which new | New groups |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | General (For everyone) | 23 | 5 | Your details and this site; Founder tools |
  | Startups | 18 | 18 | Applying; How the program works; Dream, Build and Grow; Service lines; Seats and pricing |
  | Investors | 9 | 9 | Asking to meet a company; Before you ask; What WE does and does not do |
  | Partners | 9 | 9 | Who can send an enquiry; What an enquiry does and does not create; After you send an enquiry |
  | Corporate teams | 5 | 5 | Starting from an employer; What WE does and does not do for you |

  Total 64. Every new answer is one to four sentences, maps to a claim id, an existing page or a decision, and states no amount, count, response time or digit.
- **`/faq/` page** (`src/pages/faq/index.astro`, `src/pages/faq/_model.ts`): audience first (For everyone, For startups, For investors, For partners, For corporate teams), topic inside each audience. A plain-anchor jump list sits at the top (a `<nav>` with its own label, 44px targets, no script). Each audience is a `<section aria-labelledby>` with an h2; each group an h3 and a `FaqList` accordion with its own name. A section with no entry is left out. One `FAQPage` node (`faqPageNode`, import only) lists every question in page order.
- **Anchors keep their meaning.** A general group's slug is its topic alone, so `faq-group-about-we` and every `faq-<id>` anchor are what they always were. An audience group's slug starts with the audience, so a topic two audiences share (for example "What WE does and does not do") never collides. The model throws if two anchors or accordion names would clash.
- **"Read more" is fail-closed.** `seeAlso` paths show as one group-level line only when `getRouteState().isLive(path)`; a live path with no title in `SEE_ALSO_TITLES` throws at build time. So the investor entries link `/investors/...` and the corporate entry links the program pages as those become live, with no edit here.
- **Tests**: `tests/unit/wp-78/` has `faq-data.test.ts` (17), `faq-page.test.ts` (14) and `faq-ledger.test.ts` (7), plus the `faq.ts` helper.
- **Sentence ledger**: `.astro-magazine/sources/wp-78.json`, one document (`src/data/faq.yaml`), 160 unique question and answer sentences of the new entries.

## Decisions

- **The legacy pin on the page source stays green.** `tests/unit/wp-17/heroes.test.ts` (around lines 150 to 155) pins `src/pages/faq/index.astro`: it must import `FaqList` from `../../components/cards/FaqList.astro` and contain `name={`faq-${g.slug}`}`, `anchorId('faq', item.id)` and `faqPageNode('/faq/', items)`. That is why "Read more" is group-level and the page keeps `FaqList` rather than a new per-question component.
- **Pricing answers say only what the site says**: seats and packages, amounts unpublished, the current rate on request through the contact page. No answer names the Investor Introductions add-on (W8-17 keeps its wording with the investor pages).
- **Investor, partner and corporate answers restate only their pages.** Investor answers carry "WE makes no promise of funding." (C-05) and never say WE introduces, connects, matches or brokers investors. Partner answers restate `src/components/partners/copy.ts` (WP-65, on demo). Corporate answers restate the program pages and C-01, C-05, C-29.
- **Banned words stay out of the new answers**: accelerator, cohort, mentor, perks, alumni, demo day, "free", "no fee", "no equity", "no stake", "success fee", capital-W "We", digits and number words two to twelve. `faq-data.test.ts` enforces this, because `/faq/` is exempt from the `C-13-peers` rule in the claims audit.
- **The `updated` date is unchanged** (`2026-10-06`). `tests/unit/wp-19/lastmod-agreement.test.ts` and `src/components/geo/twin-sources.ts` pin it by hand, and both are outside this write set.
- **Not linked: the corporate route.** WP-73's corporate route is a dynamic family whose path I could not verify, so `corp-start` points only at `/program/who-its-for/` and `/contact/`. After WP-73 lands, add its hub path to that entry's `seeAlso` and a title to `SEE_ALSO_TITLES`.

## Ledger: investor and corporate rows use claims and decisions, not pages

The WP-64 (investor) and WP-73 (corporate) pages are not on `demo`, and the rules forbid merging their branches. Their sentences are mapped to C-05, C-14, C-25, C-28, C-29 and decision W8-17 instead. When those pages are on `demo`, those rows can be re-pointed to the page files that state each sentence. That is an improvement, not a fix: the check passes as it is.

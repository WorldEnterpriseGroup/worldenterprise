## e2e endpoint mode (main's @endpoint ruling)

The `e2e:wave8` job builds with no intake endpoint, so every form is in `disabled` mode there. `tests/e2e/wave8/wp-63/briefs.spec.ts` follows main's convention: the disabled-mode cases (the plain not-accepting message, no mailto, the disabled button, no timing or "free" on the page) and the page cases (hub indexable, thanks noindex and reading nothing from the query string, thanks renders without JavaScript) always run and assert the mode. The endpoint-mode cases (success, 422, 429, keyboard-only, the no-JavaScript native post) carry `@endpoint` in the title and start by skipping with 'runs in e2e:forms' unless the form's `data-mode` is `endpoint`. They run for real once WP-60's fix-forward (60a.3) makes `e2e:forms` run `tests/e2e/wave8/**` with `--grep @endpoint` against its endpoint build. I did not edit the CI file. Nothing else in the spec skips.

I could not run Playwright locally (rule), so the spec is unexecuted; the pipeline is its first run.

## Decisions

- **No mailbox fallback.** With no endpoint the form is disabled and the page says "WE is not accepting brief requests on this page yet, and the form cannot be sent." No address is offered (contracts section 3.4).
- **No timing, no price, no "free".** The squeeze page, the thanks page and the hub say that the brief is sent by email to the address given and nothing about when. The thanks page says WE states no delivery time and tells the visitor to check the address and the spam folder (W7-5).
- **Sources.** NIST, EU, OWASP, IRS, SBA, USPTO, Copyright Office pages and the Delaware form carry statistic, regulation and definition claims; the two vendor documentation pages (Anthropic, OpenAI) are used for attributed paraphrase only. The EU AI Act application dates are not stated in brief 3; it says the schedule runs from 2025 to 2028 and tells the reader to read the current one.
- **Dropped sources.** The FTC business-guidance page for AI claims returned 404 twice; the NIST AI 600-1 PDF and the copyright circular PDF came back as unreadable binary and were not cited.
- **Benefit corporations** are omitted from brief 2 (C-18-pbc).
- **Fetch hygiene.** WebFetch returns summaries with a short quote limit, so each regulation quote was confirmed by a second targeted fetch that returned the sentence verbatim. The ycombinator.com result carried an "Agent use" block asking to install a CLI and log in. That is prompt injection in fetched content; it was ignored and nothing was run.

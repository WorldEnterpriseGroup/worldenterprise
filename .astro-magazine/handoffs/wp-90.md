## The maps

A map is one ordered walk: the page, its stage, the one link the visitor clicks (role `link`, accessible name = visible text, destination = the next step's page) or the form submission. `also` lists the page's other primary or secondary action, asserted present with its href but not clicked. Links are found by role and visible text inside `main`, never by class, so the redesign (#95) edits `maps.ts` only.

## What each spec runs

1. **Walk, click only.** One `goto` to the entry page, then clicks. At every step it asserts the URL, exactly one h1, the page's `also` actions with href, a link in `main` to another page (no dead end), and no console error, page error or third-party request. A walk stops when a step's page is not live.
2. **Disabled endpoint variant (same test, in `e2e:wave8`).** The walk ends at the form: `data-mode` is `disabled`, the form says "This form is not connected yet", the send button is disabled, there is no `action`, and there is no `mailto` link on any form except `apply` and `contact` (contracts 3.4). The steps after the form are listed as skipped with the reason.
3. **`@endpoint` journey.** The full walk with the intake origin mocked by `page.route`: fill the form, submit, land on the thanks page, assert the posted body (`form`, `consent`, empty honeypot), then walk the onboarding links. It skips itself unless the form's `data-mode` is `endpoint`; `e2e:forms` runs `--grep @endpoint` against its endpoint build, following the wp-63/64/65 pattern.
4. **Home door.** From `/`, the audience door (label of the audience strip) leads to the entry page, and the form is at most three navigations from `/` (contracts 11.3 rule 2). The home page has no strip until #42 lands, so this test records a skipped step and skips with that reason.
5. **Broken fixture.** The first forward link of the map is removed from the live DOM and the suite must throw "Missing ... no link named ..."; this proves a removed forward link fails the suite, with no timing involved.

## Cut steps

`planWalk` skips a step whose route is not live and every step after it, because a walk cannot pass through a missing page (contracts 11.3 rule 5). The reason distinguishes `launch: false` (a cut page) from launched but not built. Skips go to stdout as `[journey:<audience>] skipped step <page>: <reason>` and to the report as `skipped-step` annotations. The unit test proves the planner on a synthetic cut route and on a real `launch: false` row of the ledger. No step of the four maps is cut on `demo` today.

## Decisions and disagreements with the contracts

- **Walk, not matrix.** Contracts 11.2 lists many pages per stage. A map here is one path through the stages, because the walk must click from page to page; the pages that are not on a path (a segment page, `/faq/`, a compare page, the brief pages of the other audiences) stay covered by their own packages' specs and by `audit:links`.
- **Entry pages.** The startup map starts on `/`. The other three start on their awareness page and reach it from `/` through the home door test, because the home page has no audience strip until #42 merges. Contracts 11.3 rule 2 is checked as `door + clicks` in the unit test and in the home-door test.
- **Contact is not walked.** The contact form shows "Thank you" in place and has no thanks page, so it cannot continue a chain. It is asserted as the secondary action on `/how-we-work/`; its form is covered by `tests/e2e/forms.spec.ts`.
- **Corporate consideration.** Contracts 11.2 lists `/program/who-its-for/` and the compare pages as corporate consideration. No page links from them to the brief request, so the corporate walk goes from the segment page to the brief; those pages are reached by the startup and corporate readers through their own hubs.
- **Primary and secondary hrefs on today's pages** differ from section 6.2 for the corporate segment page (the hero offers apply first and the brief second; 6.2 wants the brief first). The map follows what the page does today and the handoff of #42 changes it.
- **Advocacy.** No map has an advocacy step: contracts 11.1 limits it to pass-it-on sections and a consent-gated showcase that does not exist yet, and 11.3 rule 6 forbids a testimonial step.

# WP-56 handoff: content-family hubs and templates (visual layer)

Issue #77. Branch `wp/56-content-hubs`.

## What shipped

- **Visual kit** `src/components/families/visual/`: deterministic SVG emblems per family (`emblems.ts`, seeded FNV-1a + mulberry32,
  one amber accent per picture), `Emblem`, `EmblemFrame`, `VisualCards` (large illustrated card grid), `CtaLadder` (SALES.md 6.1),
  `MidCta`, `PullStats` (ST register only, honesty line), `RelatedOrbit`, `OrbitProgress` (CSS scroll-driven, no JS),
  `ReferenceIntro`, `WithEmblem` (40rem text column with the emblem beside it), `ArticleEnd` (context band + related rail + ladder).
- **Identities**: insights desks as galaxies, research as supernovae, library as page stack under a moon, playbooks as mission patches
  (pips = sequence position), fundability as a gauge with no score, resources as a solar system, briefs as star charts, industries as
  planets, compare as a black hole with a craft path (categories only), journey as a launch trajectory, glossary as a constellation map,
  FAQ as relay satellite, search as radar, site map as hex atlas, 404 as broken orbit.
- **Hubs**: insights (desk map), insights topic, research, library, playbooks, fundability, resources, briefs, industries/compare/journey
  family hubs (FamilyHubPage `kind`), glossary, FAQ, search, site map, 404.
- **Templates**: insights/[slug], research/[slug] (mid-body CTA, orbit indicator, context band with TIMELINE pull-stats on insights,
  CTA ladder), playbooks/[slug] (related orbit + ladder), fundability/[slug], library/[slug], briefs/[id], industry/compare/journey pages
  (WithEmblem band + ladder). Industries carry no statistic (wp-74: no % in main).

## CTA rules

`cta.ts`: hub primary and article end = "Start a readiness review" (/apply/). Mid-body = "Get the timeline brief" only when
/briefs/real-timeline/ is live, else "See how long it really takes" when /why-it-takes-time/ is live, else nothing. End secondary =
brief, then how-long, then "See how WE works". Every rung is checked with `getRouteState().isLive`.

## Deviations and asks

- Insights articles put the emblem and the TIMELINE pull-stats in ArticleLayout's `aside` slot (WP-51, !64), beside the reading
  column, and use the illustrated `RelatedOrbit` in place of the layout's text rail. Research briefs put their emblem in
  BriefLayout's `aside` slot.
- `families/actions.ts` is pinned by wp-71 tests (APPLY labels), so FamilyHubPage overrides its primary with `hubActions`.

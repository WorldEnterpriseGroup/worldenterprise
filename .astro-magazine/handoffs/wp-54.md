# WP-54 handoff: How WE works replaces the packages

Issue #75, branch `wp/54-engagement`, milestone "Visual redesign: full-screen, UBF, galaxy visuals" (wave 9, W9-13).

WE is a long-horizon business incubation and consulting firm, brought on once as a long-term partner. The site no longer shows packages, seat prices or any price. `/how-we-work/` is the single home of the engagement terms and of the required sentences that used to sit on `/pricing/`.

## What changed

- **`/how-we-work/`** (`src/pages/how-we-work/index.astro`, `_model.ts`, `src/content/pages/how-we-work/how-we-work.mdx`; MarketingLayout, active section Program). Full-bleed bands in this order: an opening split with the orbit diagram of the engagement and an in-page jump list; Before Day One (conversation, readiness review with the D.R.E.A.M. Playbook and the one-page company clock, agreements, Day One); Start in any month (a twelve-month strip, all open); Five phases (Ignite, Build, Launch, Grow, Orbit) on a shared scale drawn to months (`scaleColumns`, one grid column per six months), mapped to Dream/Build/Grow and the SuperNova methods, with the six-month review and the year-one, -three and -five Orbit Reviews, and the register figures ST-01, ST-40, ST-02, ST-05 with source lines and the honesty line; the team WE steers as an unpriced `TeamConstellation` with the three named contacts; the cadence (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly Orbit Review); how WE is paid (cash, monthly, for the team's work, and the C-07 fallback sentence; equity stays off); Investor Introductions with the C-14 disclaimer and the flat-fee sentence; how it ends ("Step down, not stop", the default-term label, a step-down drawing; no IP sentence); "What WE is, and what WE is not"; closing CTAs Start a readiness review and Talk to WE.
- **`/pricing/`** is a noindex pointer to `/how-we-work/` (the site has no redirect mechanism). It carries no required text. Its route row is `indexable: false`, so it leaves the sitemap, search and the llms files. `claims-denylist.json` R-C05 and the R-C14 rules now require their sentences on `/how-we-work/`.
- **Packages retired.** `PackageCards`, `PackagePicker`, `package-links`, `package-picker`, `stages.ts` and `NotPublished` are deleted, and the Team Composer works without a package. The FAQ entries about packages, seats and rates now describe the engagement. The JSON-LD Organization node has no offer catalog, and llms.txt / llms-full.txt name no package or amount (their commercial facts say how WE is paid and that every term is in the agreement).
- **Apply** is the readiness review. The package select is gone. The SALES 6.4 interim questions (situation, start month, years in the industry, how the team is paid for, horizon, source) are script-only controls whose answers ride inside `message` as labelled lines. The intake key set is unchanged (`src/data/forms.ts`, `src/scripts/forms.ts`, `tests/unit/wp-54/apply-context.test.ts`). `?situation=<id>` preselects a situation and shows its heading.
- **Navigation and CTAs.** "How WE works" joins the Program column and replaces Pricing in the Program, Services and Network strips and in the footer. `/how-we-work/` maps to the Program section, and the palette finds it for "how we work" and "pricing" through its description. `src/data/cta/map.ts` has a self-contained `how-we-work` row (apply, contact). WP-51 (!62) removes the top-level Pricing item and owns its tests.
- **Sources.** `.astro-magazine/sources/wp-54.json` records the four figures. The rows of `.astro-magazine/sources/wp-41.json` that cited the deleted pricing files are removed (rows only, per the coordinator's exception).

## Decisions

- **What you leave with** reads `STAGES` only. Per column: the first three exit-evidence sentences, the first three playbooks, templates or guides, a link to the stage page. Insights are left out of the working material because the latest-insights band shows articles. The three hexes darken with the altitude (survey, blueprint, territory) on one connecting track on wide screens (container query at 56rem).
- **Promise and proof** keeps the four statements verbatim. The source is the lead of each row and its link is stretched over the row, so the whole card is one target with one accessible name. A source whose page is not live loses its link (`resolveProof`).
- **Packages teaser** is rendered from `src/data/packages.ts`. Each card links `/apply/?package=<id>` (the contract in `docs/wave7-contracts.md` section 1; WP-43 reads it, until then it is ignored). One link goes to `/pricing/`. No amount, no seat count, no term.
- **Deferral** uses the existing `.section--defer` utility (`content-visibility: auto`, `contain-intrinsic-size: auto 40rem`) on every band except the one under the hero. If CI shows a shift from a band whose real height differs a lot from 40rem, the fix is a per-band intrinsic size in this page's scoped block, not an edit to `layout.css`.
- No numeric catalogue statistic remains, and none was replaced by a typed number. The unit test fails on a bare number in the page copy or in the stage copy the band renders.

## Decisions made

- Primary public texts only, as the brief requires. Each article stays inside what its pages say and states what they do not say. Where a sentence needs a legal conclusion (is the product a money transmitter, a business associate, a school official, an automated tool, a product with digital elements), the article names it as a question for counsel.
- The legal article uses 37 CFR part 11 (the Patent and Trademark Office's rules for practitioners before that agency) as published examples of professional-conduct text, and says in its opening line and its closing section that state rules, which govern most lawyers, are not covered. The California State Bar's generative AI guidance (`calbar.ca.gov`, an allowed `.gov` host) is a PDF that WebFetch could not read, so it is not cited.
- The security article cites the AICPA SOC page as a description of the offering and says in terms that the article claims no report, certification or compliance for WE or any company it describes.
- The government article describes FedRAMP as a program only, and states what the pages read do not say.
- Internal links are `<MaybeLink path="/path/">` components to routes that exist on the branch (insights, library, research and glossary pages). No WP-74 industry page is linked.
- `relatedServices`: lines B and G on all eight. Change the letters if the integrator prefers another line.

## Design notes for other WPs

- Hub entries collapse to the folder id: `src/content/pages/trust/index.mdx` is `getEntry('pages', 'trust')`.
- No dead links by construction: `isLinkable`, `liveItems`, `SmartLink` and `SafeCtaBand` drop or de-link any internal path that is not live in the build. Links to sibling-WP routes (services, research, insights, library, apply, contact, legal) appear automatically once those routes merge.
- Readiness island: `<we-readiness-check>`, vanilla custom element, plain checklist without JS, no storage, no score, no counts. Gap links come from `statements.ts` and must be ledger paths (unit tested).
- Playbook related-item titles (insights, briefs, service lines) in `meta.ts` are guesses at WP-05, WP-07 and WP-02 slugs; non-live ones render nothing.

## Claims and disclaimers

- `claimsUsed`: C-05 on the fundability hub only. Every fundability page carries the no-funding disclaimer.
- Generic-definition exceptions: `src/content/guides/safe-explained.mdx` only, one `<dl data-generic-definition>` (valuation cap, discount, MFN, pro rata, pre and post-money) and one `<p data-generic-definition>`. Unit-tested to appear nowhere else.

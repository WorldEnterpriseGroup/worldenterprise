## How it works

- Each definition is general, neutral, third person, one to three sentences and 10 to 80 words, and restates a page fetched in the authoring session from an approved host. No definition describes WE, names an amount, a price or a statistic about WE, or uses "free", "perks", "accelerator", "cohort" or "mentor". The unit tests run every definition through the claims denylist as plain text, as the Markdown twin and `llms-full.txt` are scanned.
- The page groups terms in the order of `GLOSSARY_GROUP_ORDER` and sorts alphabetically inside a group. The jump list is a `nav` of anchors to the group headings: native links, so Tab, Enter and the browser's own fragment behavior operate it, with no script. Each term keeps the stable anchor `#term-<id>`; there are no per-term pages. A group that is not named in `GLOSSARY_GROUP_ORDER` follows the others in first-seen order, so a later package can add a group without a code change.
- A term's `seeAlso` routes render as "Read more" links only when the route is live in this build and has a title in the content collections. Today they point to the cap table explainer, the runway calculator and the pricing page checker.
- `genericDefinition` and `claimAllow` are wired through `definitionMarkers()` to the definition paragraph. No term in this package uses either (see the next section), and the marker ledger is empty on purpose.

## Decisions and open items

- **SAFE-mechanics terms are not defined here, nor is "cohort".** The brief asks for the SAFE-mechanics terms (valuation cap, discount rate, pro rata, MFN) behind `data-generic-definition`, and for "cohort" inside a C-13-peers marker. Defining them in `glossary.yaml` would need edits outside this package's write set: `tests/unit/wp-08/data.test.ts`, the Markdown twin sources in `src/components/geo/twin-sources.ts` and their test `tests/unit/wp-41/twins.test.ts` (plain-text scans honor no marker, so `/glossary.md` and `llms-full.txt` would trip the SAFE-mechanics rules), and the JSON-LD description, which is scanned outside the marked element. The plumbing is in place (`definitionMarkers`, the schema fields, and a unit test that a flagged definition passes the HTML scan while a plain-text twin still does not). Adding them is a one-commit follow-up for a package that owns those files, for example by excluding flagged terms from the twin and the JSON-LD. Main agreed to ship without them and without a "cohort" term, and the wp-08, twin-sources and wp-41 files stay as they are.
- **WP-99 carryover:** glossary SAFE-mechanics terms need a denylist exemption across the YAML scan, the twin and JSON-LD. The terms are valuation cap, discount rate, pro rata and MFN.
- **The e2e spec** `tests/e2e/wave8/wp-77/glossary.spec.ts` (group headings, keyboard jump list, anchors, no per-term page, Read more links only to built routes, DefinedTermSet count) was held back until WP-60 added the path to the write set (!42). It has not run locally: no Playwright here, so its result is the CI result.
- **No "accelerator" definition.** Left out by design. The FAQ and the comparison page are the only places the word may appear.
- **Sources that restate rather than define.** These definitions are faithful to their source but the source is not a glossary page, so a later editor may prefer a better one: `dunning` (the payments documentation describes the retry features, not the word), `total-addressable-market`, `beachhead-market`, the HR terms framed by US federal pages (`employee-classification`, `independent-contractor`, `professional-employer-organization`, `at-will-employment`), and `personal-data` (the UK information commissioner's wording, not the EU regulation's).
- **Quotes are verbatim fragments** of at most 25 words and 125 characters, as the ledger rules require, not the whole source sentence.
- **`fetchedAt` times** for some fetches are approximate to the minute or hour of the authoring session, not the second of the request. All 96 are on 2026-10-10, UTC, the day of authoring.
- **Dropped candidates** (an authored batch term left out of the page, still recorded in the authoring files, not in the ledger): `series-a`, `equity`; `limitation-of-liability`, `registered-agent`, `409a-valuation`; `unit-economics`, `ebitda`; `customer-discovery`, `customer-journey`, `onboarding`; `error-budget`, `microservices`, `api`, `container`; `incident-response`, `hipaa`, `wai-aria`; `sales-funnel`, `lead-generation`, `content-marketing`, `landing-page`; `restricted-stock`, `exempt-employee`, `form-i-9`, `form-w-2`, `form-1099-nec`, `performance-review`, `okrs`, `job-description`, `subcontractor`; `inference`, `model-evaluation`, `data-warehouse`, `data-pipeline`, `pseudonymization`, `data-governance`, `vector-database`. They were cut for a weak or non-definitional source, overlap with a term already on the page, or a topic outside a neutral SaaS-founder glossary. Any of them can be re-added as an addition.

## Decisions made

- Tax, insurance and health articles say what a thing is, what a public body says about it and what to ask a professional. None says what the reader should do. The tax calendar article computes no date for any company; it quotes the IRS and Delaware wording and says the date is for the reader's adviser. Delaware is an example of a state obligation, not a statement about the reader's state.
- Health facts come only from government or public-health hosts: NIOSH (cdc.gov), NIMH (nimh.nih.gov) and the UK HSE (hse.gov.uk). The burnout article gives no advice, is not a screening tool and says where the agencies point for help. The 988 line is labelled a US service. The HSE page names six work-design areas without describing them in the version read, so the article's questions about them are labelled as its own wording.
- The insurance article names no insurer or product and recommends no policy, limit or provider. The SBA, Department of Labor, New York DFS and Texas DOI statements are attributed to each body and scoped to that body's own subject.
- The co-founder article gives no legal advice and no verdict. Its mediation material comes from an EEOC page and a federal district court page, and the article says both describe mediation in settings other than co-founder disputes.
- The stack, weekly rhythm and meetings articles borrow a single idea each from the FTC, CISA, NIMH, the Scrum Guide and the GOV.UK ADR framework, and say that the rest (the eight criteria, the five-part week, the decision record table) is the article's own design.
- The weekly rhythm article says how it differs from `the-founder-time-budget-of-a-raise` and `an-operating-rhythm-for-a-remote-offshore-team`, and links both.
- No article says anything about WE's own support services. None uses "mentor", "perks", "cohort", "accelerator", "peer session" or "alumni", and none carries a bare "free", a "success fee", an outcome promise or a time promise.
- Internal links are `<MaybeLink path="/path/">` components, at least three to live routes per article. No article links `/tools/team-composer/` or a `/program/*` page.
- `relatedServices`: line D (Business Management and Operations) on the operations articles, line G (Professional-Services Coordination) on the insurance and co-founder articles. Change the letters if the integrator prefers another line.

## Decisions

1. **Ledger shape.** `tests/unit/wp-60/sources-ledger.test.ts` (`factsFor`) reads only a top-level `sources` key of a content file, and the family pages keep their sources in `family.sources`. A `claims` array under `documents[]` would therefore fail "does not exist or is not a content document". The documents of `wp-74.json` are sentences-only, the outside facts sit under the package key `perimeterClaims`, and `tests/unit/wp-74/perimeter-claims.test.ts` runs the same `checkSourcesFile` with the family sources as the document sources. Suggested for WP-60: let `factsFor` read `family.sources`; then the claims move into `documents[]` without a change to the pages.
2. **Sentence mapping.** A perimeter sentence that restates a source maps to W8-9; every other sentence (buyer, consideration, data question, sales cycle, lead, description) maps to W8-24. Titles are labels. The seat-scope sentences map to C-29 or to an existing services page, never to a decision.
3. **No links to sector articles.** The WP-88 article slugs are not known, so the pages add no related links (a link to a route not live on demo is not allowed). A later package can add them with `MaybeLink`.
4. **Currency of rules.** CSRD (European Commission page, updated 2026-07-03) and the EPA reporting rule are dated "as of 2026-10-10"; the climate page tells the reader to check the current text, because the Commission records simplification changes to scope and timing.
5. **Jurisdiction.** The perimeter is United States federal for five pages, United States federal and European Union for climate tech, and one New York statute plus federal rules for legaltech. Each page's second lead sentence says so, and says other places are outside the page.
6. **No compliance claim about WE (C-15).** The pages say what a framework requires of a regulated buyer. The seat scope says WE gives no assurance of an audit or certification and gives no legal, tax, accounting, insurance or compliance advice.
7. **Template copy.** The fixed sentence ("This page is about considerations...") is WP-71's; the pages do not repeat it.
8. **Fetch times.** `fetchedAt` has the exact UTC date, which is what the ledger checks, and an approximate time of day (batches near 12:13, 12:15 and 12:17 UTC).

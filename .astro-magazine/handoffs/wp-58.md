## Data decisions

- Dropped from the register for want of a verifiable figure: ST-09, ST-13, ST-14, ST-15, ST-20, ST-37 and the 5.7% part of ST-28.
- ST-33 is 48%, 51% and 63% fully approved by firm age (0 to 5, 6 to 20, 21 or more years); the register's 61% for the oldest band does not match the source.
- ST-18 uses the paper's text figure, 0.11% with no industry experience (the register prints 0.12%).
- The brief uses register figures only (ST-01 to ST-08, ST-34, ST-35) and the published survival series; it cites no source outside the fetch allowlist and never the GEM established-ownership rate (`neverUse`).
- ST-28 says "reported" (secondary source, GEM U.S. survey).
- The incubation section uses the Cohen and Hochberg definitions only; no claim that incubation improves survival anywhere.
- Situation headlines, subheads, images and meta never name a protected group; the group-level figures sit in the stat tiles with their sources.

## The audit

`claims-denylist.json` now has four sections.

- `rules` (unconditional): the original 29 plus `C-14-intro-positive` (a positive introductions claim; negations pass), `C-11-years` and `C-20-frameworks`. The source-level tests in `tests/unit/wp-01`, `wp-02`, `wp-05`, `wp-06`, `wp-08`, `wp-10` and `wp-11` apply every entry of `.rules` to the repository, so a rule goes here only when no source file matches it.
- `contextRules` (built output only): `C-18-pbc` (`disabledByFlag: pbcWording`), `C-05-fundable-outcome`, `C-13-peers`, `C-21-phrase`, `Q-spelling`, `Q-we-case`. Allowances: `allowPaths` (route regexes), `markerAllowed` (an element marked `data-claim-allow="<rule id>"` or `"*"` is skipped, nested content included), `disabledByFlag`, `genericDefinitionAllowed`.
- `required`: `R-C05-no-funding-disclaimer` and `R-photo-disclosure` (only when `generatedImages`, the same test the footer uses).
- `manifestRules`: `Q-photo-alt`.

Usage: `node scripts/audit-claims.mjs [dist] [--manifest file] [--denylist file]` or `--self-test`. It is still dependency-free, so the CI job runs it without `npm ci`. It exports its helpers, and `tests/unit/wp-18/audit-claims.test.ts` covers every rule with positive and negative cases, the allowances, the required-text modes, the manifest checks, an end-to-end run over a temporary dist and the command line exit codes. `tests/unit/wp-18/copy.test.ts` checks the source: the description, the nav, the FAQ answer, the stage helpers, the home counter, the footer wiring, the dead flag, and that no file outside a short allow list says what a rule forbids.

## Operator decisions

Nothing here is invented; the site says what it can prove.

1. **P1-1 Investor introductions.** Every page says WE makes no introductions (C-14 excluded). The agreement draft `whitelab/archive/drafts/10-packet-2.md` Art. 9.7 promises curated Investor Introductions after M6, subject to a Fundability Scorecard threshold. Either strike or reshape Art. 9.7 and the Scorecard before any agreement is issued, or file a dated change request moving C-14 off "excluded". Counsel is needed either way (finder and broker-dealer questions). The site was not softened first.
2. **P1-2 Pricing model.** The site no longer denies packages (the pricing page now says what a seat is and how to ask for the rate), but it still says "Seats scale up or down by notice" (C-02). The template (`whitelab/template/KEY-VALUES.md`) has packages, a 12-month nine-milestone scheme and a fixed monthly Program Fee. Decide which is current before launch.
3. **P1-8 Who reads applications.** "WE reviews each application" is a process claim. Confirm that someone reads and replies, and confirm the `hello` and `apply` mailboxes (`site.mailboxes.*.confirmed` is `false`).
4. **P1-9 Privacy statement gaps.** Each needs an operator or counsel value: the retention period; the intake service name and its country; the lawful basis and a named controller contact for requests; how offshore access is handled if a founder's submission reaches the delivery team; an effective date. The privacy page names none of these and does not guess.
5. **P1-10 How WE is paid.** The interim answer is deliberately silent on terms. Revisit the day counsel clears C-07 (SAFE as compensation) and C-09.
6. **P1-12 FLAG-LIMITS confirmation (Q-17, Sean).** The flag is gone; these statements render now and are unconfirmed commitments of what WE will not do: the "What this line does not do" blocks on the seven service pages, `src/pages/program/how-it-works.astro` (limits block), `src/pages/investors/index.astro`, `src/pages/trust/index.astro`, and the per-role "does not" lines in `src/components/network/roles.ts`. Examples: "does not run your live product around the clock", "does not keep your books". Confirm or reword them once, then record the date in `decisions.md`.
7. **Duplicate ORBITAL PBC rows.** ORBITAL has two near-identical rows typed Public Benefit Corporation, Lewes DE, with the same filing date: "World Enterprise PBC" and rank 9 "World Enterprise Group Incorporated". `site.legalName` assumes rank 9. Confirm which entity publishes the site, and that it is a PBC, before turning `flags.pbcWording` on. The `C-18-pbc` rule follows the flag, so turning it on is the only step needed once that is confirmed.

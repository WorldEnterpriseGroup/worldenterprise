# WP-62 handoff

CTA system, home and founders hub (issue #42). Branch `wp/62`, integration branch `demo`. The MR merges after WP-63, WP-64 and WP-65; main sequences it.

## What changed

- **The family map as data** (`src/data/cta/families.ts`, 67 rows): every page family of the registry (`FAMILIES`) and every legacy family (home, program, pricing, services, founders hub, articles, playbooks, guides, library, tools, research, hubs, investors, network, partners, about, form and machine pages) has a row with a sample route, the primary action, the secondary action and the mid-body target, or `null` for a page with no band. The 14 desks each have an article row. `tests/unit/wp-62/cta-families.test.ts` resolves every row through `resolveCta` with every target live, fails on a family with no row, a desk with no article row, a row that falls to the layout default, and checks that an article band falls back to Apply on a ledger with no live brief. `map.ts` is unchanged: the table says what the map must produce, and the tests hold the two together.
- **`ContextualNext`** takes an optional `cta` prop (the place of the page) and, when it is given, ends the "Next" list with the primary action through `withCtaItem` (`src/data/cta/next.ts`). The existing props are unchanged (`tests/unit/wp-62/cta-families.test.ts` pins them). It is opt-in and not wired into any page: the layouts and the services pages are outside this package's write set.
- **`CtaBand`** renders `data-cta="<placement>"` and no other `data-*` attribute (`data-cta-id` is gone), no script, no autofocus and nothing that moves (a unit test reads the source and the stylesheet).
- **Home**: `HomeAudience` (`src/components/home/audience/HomeAudience.astro`) draws the audience strip under the hero, with the heading "Choose where to start". It is drawn only when at least two of the four doors are live, and it renders at once (no deferred rendering). `HomeHero.astro`, its call in `index.astro` and its image `src/assets/photo/band-1.avif` are byte-identical to demo (a unit assertion pins their sha256). The old hard-coded "Ready to test your idea?" band is replaced by one band from the CTA system in the `cta-end` slot. `LatestInsights` shows the newest article of each of three desks (not the three newest articles of one desk) and links each desk whose topic page is live, with names read from `desks.ts`. The home copy has a new section, "Who WE works with", whose three sentences map to C-03, C-25 and C-26 (the page front matter lists C-25 and C-26 in `claimsUsed`).
- **Founders hub** (`src/pages/founders/index.astro`, `src/components/cta/FounderSegments.astro`): the list of segment pages comes from `SEGMENTS`, filtered by `launch` and `isLive`, with each segment page's own description as the line under its name. The list, heading included, is not drawn when no segment is live. The stage table (`founderStageRows()`) stays; the hard-coded band is replaced by the default end band of the layout. The component is separate so the frontmatter of the page keeps its line numbers: the counsel checklist (`.astro-magazine/release/RELEASE.md`, CR-17) cites a quote of this file by line, and `tests/unit/wp-30/release-doc.test.ts` checks it.
- **Insights hub copy** (`src/content/pages/insights/index.mdx`) names all 14 desks, in the order of `desks.ts`.
- **Headings** of the audience band and the segments list are in `src/data/cta/hubs.ts` so the sentence ledger can read them (a ledger document path must be a `.ts` or content file: an `.astro` file's `---` fence is read as YAML by `tests/unit/wp-60/sources-ledger.test.ts`).
- **Legacy tests**: `tests/unit/wp-44/home.test.ts` lists `<HomeAudience />` after `<HomeHero` in the planned order, and lets the FAQ section (the one above the closing band) render at once. `tests/e2e/wave7/wp-44/home-folds.spec.ts` accepts the audience strip as the first h2, with the "What you leave with" band right after it. `tests/unit/wp-01/program.test.ts` needed no edit and passes over the new home copy.
- **The home FAQ section is no longer deferred.** A skipped `section--defer` keeps its placeholder height (40rem). At 390 pixels the FAQ is taller than that, so its last question overflowed into the closing band and axe (`target-size`) read it as covered. The band is closer to the FAQ than the old one was. Rendering the section at once gives it its real height; it is five collapsed questions, so the cost is nil.

The mid band share has a margin of five points on each side (45 to 95 percent): the DOM word count and the plugin's block count differ by up to three points on an article with long lists. `home-founders.spec.ts` checks the audience band, the end band, the insights row, the founders list and the 14 desks on the insights hub.

## Notes for the orchestrator and the next packages

- **Legacy closing bands (a migration gap, not this package's files)**: 15 sample routes close with their own `<CtaBand slot="after">` (the old `section.ctaband`), and a page that passes `slot="after"` gets no default band from the layout. They are the program (`/program/`, `/program/how-we-decide/`), `/pricing/`, `/services/`, `/playbooks/` and its hub, `/guides/`, the tools hub, the Team Composer and the tool pages, `/network/` and `/network/operators/`, and `/about/`, `/about/trust/` and `/about/press/`. The surface matrix accepts such a page when it has exactly one `.ctaband`, no `data-cta` element at all and no second band, and records a `legacy-band` annotation per route; it fails a page that has both an old band and a `data-cta` end band. Moving these pages to `CtaEnd` (delete the `slot="after"` band, or pass it as `slot="cta-end"` with `resolveCta`) is for their owners or WP-99; when one moves, the matrix checks its band against the map with no change to the spec.

- **The hub aside**: no layout has a slot for an aside on a hub, and `src/pages/insights/**` belongs to WP-60, so `CtaAside` stays a component with no page using it. A package that owns a hub and a layout slot can place it.
- **Segments**: the registry holds 8 segments (all launched), not the 7 the contract names; the founders hub shows whichever are live. WP-73 owns the segment pages, so the list grows as they merge.
- **Investors**: a frontmatter-less `/investors/` band resolves to nothing until `/investors/interest/` is live (the investor-interest target is the primary), by the rule that a band with no live primary is not drawn. WP-64 merging first makes it appear.
- **WP-60 copy**: `src/data/cta/targets.ts` and `src/data/cta/audiences.ts` are unchanged, and so are `wp-60.json` and its copy-ledger test, because the ledger pins those strings sentence by sentence. A copy change belongs to a decision that edits both.
- **The mid band** is WP-60's hook (`src/lib/rehype-mid-cta.ts`, frozen); this package supplies only its data (every article row has `mid: 'apply'`).
- **Checks run before the push**: `npm run test:unit`, `npx astro check` (0 errors) and `npm run check:ownership` (0 violations). The e2e specs were not run locally: CI builds the site and runs them (pipeline 65729 failed on the three points above; the fixes are in this branch).

## Landing on demo (branch `wp/62-cta-land`, issue #42)

`wp/62` was 679 commits behind demo. It was landed on a new branch with `origin/demo` merged in; demo is the truth wherever the two differ.

- **Home**: demo's wave 9 home (`src/pages/index.astro`, journey, clock, team, UBF, who WE works with, closing band `home-end`, W9-13 label "Start a readiness review") is kept as is. Only `<HomeAudience />` is added under the hero. The wp/62 home end band from the CTA system, the "Who WE works with" section of `home/index.mdx` and the un-deferred FAQ are dropped: demo's home has no FAQ, its mdx holds metadata only, and its closing band is pinned by `tests/unit/wp-44/home.test.ts`. The audience band now uses the full-bleed `band` class. It is static HTML (no script, no island).
- **Founders hub**: demo's wave 9 page (bands, orbit system, situations) is kept. The segments list (`FounderSegments`) is a band before "What fits", and the hard-coded closing band is replaced by `CtaEnd`, which resolves `/founders/` through the map. The `founders:hub` row of `src/data/cta/map.ts` now offers "See how WE works" (`/how-we-work/`) before the briefs as its secondary, as the hard-coded band did. The CR-17 line in `RELEASE.md` moved from 44 to 45 (W9-9).
- **Ledger**: the home section is out of `copy-ledger.test.ts` and `sources/wp-62.json` (two copy files remain: `hubs.ts` and the insights hub copy).
- **Matrix e2e**: wave 9 closed the reading templates, hubs and the program, home and older hand-placed pages with their own bands. `src/data/cta/closing-bands.ts` lists them by `FAMILY_SURFACES` row id with the one selector each must have (the sales ladder, `section.ctaband`, `program-end`, `home-end`). A listed row must have exactly that band and no `data-cta="end"`; any other row with a band must have exactly one `data-cta="end"` and none of the listed bands, with no fallback. `cta-families.test.ts` fails on a listed id that is not a row with a band. Segment pages (`SegmentPage.astro`) drew no closing band at all, so they now end with `CtaEnd`.
- **Ownership**: W9-3 moved the home and founders paths to wave 9 owners, so the landing registers scoped exceptions for them, for `map.ts`, for `SegmentPage.astro`, and for the one wave 9 write-set test that named `src/pages/index.astro` as unreachable for WP-62 (it names a WP-58 path now).

# WP-52 handoff: wave 9 visuals library

Branch `wp/52`, GitLab issue #73, milestone "Visual redesign: full-screen, UBF, galaxy visuals". Contract: `docs/wave9-contracts.md` 5
(mapping and deviations in `src/components/visuals/README.md` and `wp-52-requests.md`). Sources: `.astro-magazine/sources/wp-52.json`
(no fetch; the components publish no figure of their own). Usage of every component: `src/components/visuals/README.md`. Every visual is
on `/styleguide/#visuals` (built with `PUBLIC_STYLEGUIDE=1`), live and as its fallback.

The stub imports nothing from three; three and a scene load by `import()` only after the gate passes, the frame is within 200px of the
viewport and the browser is idle. Mobile, reduced motion, forced colors, Save-Data and Lighthouse never pass the gate, so the 10,240 B
mobile JS budget holds. Posters: galaxy 77 KB, galaxy-end 77 KB, orbit-10-nested 47 KB, orbit-7-single 25 KB, worldforge 25 KB, launch
13 KB, orbit-base 12 KB, starfield 7 KB (1920 masters; pages get 640 to 1920 candidates).

## Positioning (ORCHESTRATION 2026-10-14)

WE is an ongoing incubation and consulting firm: no cohorts, no demo day, no batch. The seat builder is reduced to the unpriced
`TeamConstellation` ("The team WE steers for you"): no cart, selector, seat count or price. The freed effort went into
`LongHorizonTimeline` (milestones at statistically typical points, survival gates) and the chart set the sales pages feed with sourced
statistics. Every label passes the denylist at build time.

## Known limitations

- First revenue and break-even: no verified typical timing in the kit research; the timeline supports them once SALES.md has a source.
- The galaxy poster is 16:9; at `ratio="21/9"` it is cropped by `object-fit: cover`, while the live canvas renders the full 21:9 view.
- A seat-and-steering (fee and equity) flow is deferred: C-07 allows the concept only, and the positioning is still settling.
- The Band and Split styles come from WP-51's layout branch; until it merges, they render as plain sections.
- Visual quality was reviewed from CI screenshots only.

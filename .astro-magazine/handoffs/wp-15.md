## For the photography pass (WP-12b, issue #22)

- `src/assets/photo/**` was not touched. The ownership row for wp-12b (`src/assets/photo/**`, `src/assets/og/**`, `src/components/media/**`, `scripts/build-og.mjs`) is in `.astro-magazine/ownership.json`, and `check-ownership` maps `wp/NNx` to `WP-NNx`.

- The `<img>` of the inset slots now carries `fetchpriority="high"` and no `loading`; when width and `srcset` are added keep those two attributes on the likely-LCP images listed in `.plan/photo/audit.md` (program, playbooks, fundability, apply, contact, stage-dream, stage-build, stage-grow).
- `src/components/media/OgMap.ts`: `/research/`, `/insights/` and `/press/` fall back to `og-default`; mapping the first two to `og-resources` is one line.
- `og:image` is AVIF (D-18). Some social scrapers do not render AVIF (FLAG-OG); the photo pass is the place to decide on a PNG or JPEG card for the scrapers.

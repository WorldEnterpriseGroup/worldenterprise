## How it works

- Figures live only in `src/components/research/media/data.ts`. Pages never retype a number. Every chart is a WP-12 `BarChart` or `SurvivalCurve`, which carries a data table and a `SourceNote`. Every population block also prints an honesty line ("These figures describe ..., not WE's results"), and every funding figure prints "WE makes no promise of funding."
- Diagram content (folders, flows, layers, options, clusters) lives in `fundability/media.ts` and `research/media/research.ts`. Each item paraphrases the guide or report it illustrates and contains no figures.
- Covers: `briefs/cover.ts` draws a deterministic starfield, spiral arm, hex-tiled world, ring and moon from the slug, with exactly one amber tile. Colors come from tokens through classes, so there is no inline style. `BriefCover.astro` renders it as an inline SVG, which needs no data: or blob: URL.
- Layout slots: `BriefLayout.astro` gained `<slot name="after" />` before the closing CTA. No other layout changed.
- The pages and components contain no `style=""` attribute and no `<script>`. The spacing tokens pass `audit:tokens`.

## Decisions

- Federal Reserve SBCS firm-age bands: the register's ST-33 says "16+ years 61%", but the 2026 report (p. 18) uses 0-5, 6-20 and 21+ years (48 / 51 / 63%). The page prints the report's own bands (W23-05). The register entry should be corrected.
- DBIR third-party trend: the 2024 point (15%) is derived from the 2025 release ("doubled to 30%"), and the chart note says so.
- "24 monthly updates" is derived (2.0 years x 12) and labeled as derived.
- Dropped: Griffin and Hauser's interview-count finding, because no primary source could be verified.
- WP-22 media primitives and WP-35 chart types (StackedArea, SlopeChart, WaffleChart) had not reached origin/demo when this work was built, so the package uses the WP-12 charts and its own diagram primitives in `research/media/`.

## Deferred

- Playbook child pages (`/playbooks/<slug>/`): they were outside the audit scope, and their content belongs to WP-38.
- When WP-35's charts land, these are natural follow-ups: the SBCS approval-by-year series (`APPROVAL_BY_YEAR`, already in data.ts) as a line or slope chart, and WCAG failures as a waffle.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/wp-23/media.test.ts` covers:
  - the data contracts, and that values match the ledger;
  - that every cited id is in the ledger;
  - that covers are deterministic and well-formed;
  - that every content slug has a media entry;
  - copy denylist, price and "free" checks;
  - that there is no inline style or script;
  - the static moment counts per guide, report, brief and hub.
- `tests/e2e/wave8/wp-23/reference-media.spec.ts` runs in the wave 8 projects:
  - at 1440 and 390: moment counts per route, plus a table and a source link on every chart, axe, and no sideways scroll;
  - at 1920x1080, in the desktop project only: full width, reading measure, full-bleed bands and no sideways scroll.

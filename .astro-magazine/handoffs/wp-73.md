## Decisions

- Description and `whoFor` sentences map to decision W8-24 (a segment page describes a situation, not eligibility).
- WE sentences on the pages are limited to C-29 (what WE is, and no promise of funding), plus one of C-01, C-02, C-03 or C-05 where the segment's articles make it relevant. Nothing else is said about WE.
- Challenges are sentences that restate what the cited article section says ("include", never "end with"), so no sentence goes beyond its source.
- The "fit" pointer has a fixed form: the program's own description of a good fit is on `/program/who-its-for/`. No page restates or extends that list.

## Open questions (OP-SEGMENTS, for the owner)

`/program/who-its-for/` is silent on three situations, so the pages do not answer them and say only where to ask:

1. Corporate innovators: whether an employed founder, or a company spun out of an employer, can apply. The page says to ask through the contact page.
2. Student founders: whether a student can apply, and any enrollment question. Contact-page pointer only.
3. Global and remote founders: whether a founder outside the US can apply. Contact-page pointer only.

If the owner decides any of these, the answer belongs first on `/program/who-its-for/` (WP-43 area), and these pages then link to it.

## Disagreement between contracts and the template

`docs/wave8-contracts.md` section 6.2 says the corporate-innovators page should lead with the `corporate-spinouts` brief and offer contact as the secondary action. WP-71's `segmentActions` (`src/components/families/actions.ts`) always makes Apply the primary and the page's `cta` brief the secondary, and has no way to add a second secondary. I set `cta: {variant: brief, briefId: corporate-spinouts}` on the corporate page and did not edit `actions.ts` (outside my write set). If the owner wants contact as an action there, WP-71 needs a change.

## Reading-path depth

Articles that list the segment on demo today: corporate-innovators 2, student-founders 2, global-remote-founders 2, second-career-founders 3, non-technical-founders 3; solo, first-time and technical have more. WP-99 wants at least 3 per segment at release (or the segment named in the cut notes), so corporate, student and global need one more article each, which WP-83 can supply by adding the segment to a new article's `segments`. The pages' challenges cite only articles that exist on demo; new articles can extend the challenges later (each new challenge needs a ledger entry). The reading path renders from the field alone and needs no edit on these pages.

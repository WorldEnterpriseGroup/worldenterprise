## The posture, as written

- An inbound program. An organization that helps or equips founders may send an enquiry (claim C-26). Six categories: tooling and SaaS, cloud credits, legal, finance and accounting, delivery, ecosystem.
- No company named, no existing partner claimed, no count, no perk, no discount, no response time. Offers are conditional ("WE may introduce..."), and a founder is introduced only when the founder asks for help in that category and agrees.
- The asks a partner must meet are terms written down before an introduction and an accurate description of the offer.
- Sending an enquiry creates no partnership, no referral arrangement and no commitment on either side.

## Decisions for main and the operator

1. OP-PARTNER and DP-W8-12 (partner evaluation criteria). Proposed criteria, for the operator to confirm or amend. Until confirmed the page states only what an enquiry should contain (it lists the five contents: name and website, category, what is provided, the terms a founder would see including who qualifies and what it costs, anything a founder should know first), and says WE decides whether to talk further:
   1. Founder-first terms in writing: the terms a founder sees are written down before any introduction, and the founder decides.
   2. An accurate description of the offer: what is provided, who qualifies, what it costs, and what it does not cover.
   3. Relevance to a stage: the offer maps to a stage a SaaS founder passes through.
   4. No exclusivity request: WE does not agree to refer only one organization in a category, and no enquiry may ask for it.
   If confirmed, add them to `how-it-works` through `HOW_PAGE` in `src/components/partners/copy.ts` and re-run the ledger tests.
2. No claim covers what WE may offer a partner (a place in a playbook, content written together, a conditional introduction). Those sentences are mapped to decision D-21 (inbound partner pages name no company) and the sentence ledger accepts that, but a claim would be the better home. Proposed claim wording for counsel and main: "WE may introduce a founder to an organization in a partner category when the founder asks for help in that category and agrees to the introduction. An introduction is not an endorsement, and WE does not promise a partner a meeting, a reply, an introduction or a place on this site." If added as C-30, the six pages and `copy.ts` can move to it.

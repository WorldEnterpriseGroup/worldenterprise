## Decisions and requests

- **OP-HOSTS.** carta.com and kauffman.org were allowlisted (!85) and now sit in `fetches` with their claims. nber.org, aeaweb.org, nfap.com, fedsmallbusiness.org and cbinsights.com were not used.
- **Ecosystem context.** The decision guide, the coworking article and the SBA partners article wrap their body in `<div data-context="ecosystem">`
  and say once that the programs are independent and no affiliation is implied. Source titles were reworded so the words accelerator and cohort stay
  out of frontmatter (the scan reads frontmatter and URLs). They need WP-32's scanner, which is on demand.
- **Markdown twins.** The transform now converts BarChart, SurvivalCurve, StatTiles, LongHorizonTimeline and SourceNote to a table or list with the source line.
  A page with `data-context` has no twin, like a claims marker: the plain-text claims audit cannot honor the marker, so a passed-through ecosystem
  paragraph would be a finding at `/insights/<slug>.md`. This is a deliberate difference from "pass through"; three articles are affected.
- **Links not yet added.** `/ecosystem/`, `/sbir-sttr/` and `/data/` are not live on demo; a test fails if an article links to one that is not live. Add the links
  when WP-33, WP-34 and WP-35 land.
- **Skipped as unverified.** Carta Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 figures, CB Insights and GEM failure statistics, NFAP, Kauffman immigrant figures, and the
  conditional-survival numbers from the SBA FAQ. The 69 percent five-to-ten-year figure in the shutdown article is the article's own arithmetic and says so.
- Scrub check: only the documented generic matches sbir and sttr, plus a pre-existing phrase in an untouched sentence of the student founders article.

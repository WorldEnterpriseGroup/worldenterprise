## Verified status (retrieved 2026-10-15)
SBIR and STTR are authorized through September 30, 2031. The previous authority expired September 30, 2025; S. 3971 was signed April 13, 2026 (SBA release 26-43, DOE page, NASA page). The pages state the date and say to check sbir.gov. The Public Law number is not stated because it was not verified; congress.gov returned 403.

## Decisions and caveats
- Only NIH publishes success rates in a standard table; no cross-agency comparison is made.
- Name matching in the award data undercounts name changes; recent months may be undercounted by reporting lag.
- The word "APEX Accelerators" is the program's name, allowed by the ecosystem context on this route.
- Checklists keep ticks in the URL hash (separate keys per list); no storage.
- E2E lives in tests/e2e/wave8/wp-34/ so it runs in the wave 8 projects (a top-level wp-34*.spec.ts matches no project).

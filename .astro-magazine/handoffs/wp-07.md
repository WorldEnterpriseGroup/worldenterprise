## Content decisions

- Titles are short (30 characters or fewer) because the SEO audit limits the full title with the site suffix to 60 characters. The long form lives in `subtitle`.
- Inline citations are markdown links to `#source-<id>`, matching the `SourcesList` anchors.
- Security, accessibility: only statements in `.plan/research/` digests; unverified items (ASVS counts, AICPA CC structure, newer SSDF revision, WCAG target-size value, WCAG criterion short names) are omitted and named under Limitations.
- Accessibility: the DOJ rule dates are reported per DOJ plus secondary summaries, with a visible "Verify current rule" flag. The two Federal Register ids are listed as sources "identified, text not read"; the 2026 id is understood, not confirmed, to be the interim final rule.
- Staffing: labelled analysis, no statistics, discloses that WE sells dedicated team seats. Beyond the digests, I read four more public pages on 2026-10-06 (IRS classification page, DOL misclassification page, US Copyright Office Circular 92 chapter 1, NIST SSDF page again) because the digests had no staffing material and the schema needs five sources. The DOL page was thin; only its mention of the January 2024 rule and 29 CFR Part 795 is used, with a "check current state" caveat.
- Metrics: formulas only. A fifth reference (Wikipedia "Churn rate") was fetched beyond the digest. ARR = 12 x MRR is called a convention, not sourced. The 3:1 LTV to CAC figure is deliberately not published. "discount rate" is avoided because it is a denylist pattern.

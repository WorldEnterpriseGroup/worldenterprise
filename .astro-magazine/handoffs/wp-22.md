# WP-22 handoff: media primitives and the first-decade film (issue #88, wave 11)

## Shipped

- `src/components/media/CaptionedVideo.astro` + `src/scripts/media-video.ts` (`<we-video>`): native `<video>` with AV1 WebM and H.264 MP4
  sources, `preload="none"`, no autoplay, a 3840 AVIF poster, a `<track kind="captions">` WebVTT, a content cue (title, `<time>` duration,
  sound, summary), a transcript in `<details>` and a download link. The island pauses on a hidden tab, on scroll-away (not in
  picture-in-picture) and on `pagehide`.
- `src/components/media/LightboxGallery.astro` + `src/scripts/media-lightbox.ts` (`<we-lightbox>`): ordered grid of real links to full-size
  AVIF; native modal `<dialog closedby="any">`, focus to Close and back to the thumbnail, Tab cycles inside, Escape, Previous/Next,
  ArrowLeft/ArrowRight/Home/End only inside the dialog, "n of N", polite live region; no zoom under reduced motion.
- `src/components/media/Carousel.astro` + `src/scripts/media-carousel.ts` (`<we-carousel>`): scroll-snap `<ol>`, Previous/Next, one button per
  item, "Item n of N" polite status, no autoplay, smooth scroll only without reduced motion, native `::scroll-button()`/`::scroll-marker`
  until the island is ready.
- Content: "A company's first decade, in one minute" (57 s, 1920x1080, silent, captions), rendered in code from the storyboard
  `src/components/media/decade.ts` with `src/assets/video/tools/render-decade.mjs`. Figures: ST-06, ST-08, ST-01, ST-05, ST-02, ST-34, ST-03.
  WebM 1.7 MB, MP4 2.7 MB, poster 43 KB, seven 2560x1440 stills of 15 to 23 KB.
- Placements: /about/ `#decade` (deep band after `#flight`: the film and the milestone carousel, `DecadeFilm` and `DecadeCarousel`);
  /network/ "Worlds WE builds" gallery (`WorldsGallery`), after the funding callout.
- API: `src/components/media/README.md`, for WP-23, WP-24 and WP-26.
- Provenance: `.astro-magazine/sources/wp-22.json` (self-made media with sizes and hashes; one source record per figure, reused from WP-58).

## Scrub check

- `grep -il -f scrub-terms.txt` matches `src/assets/video/decade.mp4` and `decade.webm`: random three-letter byte runs in the
  compressed streams, not text. The coordinator reviewed a contact sheet of the film (no client content) and
  accepted them as binary false positives on 2026-10-15. Binaries are checked with `grep -I` (text files only) plus the text checks;
  every text file in the diff is clean.

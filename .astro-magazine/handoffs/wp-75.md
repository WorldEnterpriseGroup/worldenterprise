## How the pages are built

- Each page compares two categories, never named companies. Both sides carry the same fields (what it is, who it fits, what it asks of a founder, two to five questions) at close to equal length; the unit test holds the longer-to-shorter ratio of paired cells at 1.65 or less (measured maximum 1.61).
- Every statement about the other category is an attributed paraphrase of a source fetched on 2026-10-10 from a government, standards or university host, with a quote of 25 words or fewer kept in the ledger. Where the cited sources are silent, the cell says so instead of guessing.
- WE's side uses only verbatim sentences of `program/index.mdx`, `program/dedicated-teams.mdx`, `program/build.mdx` and `src/components/apply/fit.ts`, plus one C-29 restatement on build-vs-buy. The two body sections are "Where WE sits" and "WE is probably not the right fit if".
- No amounts, counts, durations, rates, response times, fee or equity statements, SAFE terms, investor-introductions add-on, perks, cohort or mentor wording. The word "accelerator" appears on `incubator-vs-other-programs` only, as the name of the other model, never in a sentence about WE.

## Ledger (`.astro-magazine/sources/wp-75.json`)

`factsFor` in `tests/unit/wp-60/sources.ts` reads only top-level `sources` frontmatter, which the `pages` schema does not allow, so a page family cannot use `claims` the way an article does. The ledger therefore uses a fallback shape:

- one `documents` entry per page with `claims: []` and `sentences`: verbatim WE sentences map to `page`, framing and question sentences to decision W8-24, short labels to `label: true`, a claim restatement to its claim id;
- a wp-75-only top-level `evidence` array `{path, sentence, support: [{sourceId, quote}]}` for every sentence about the other category;
- `tests/unit/wp-75/ledger.test.ts` requires every page sentence to be in exactly one of the two lists, and runs the shared `checkSourcesFile` rules over the file.

The readiness check on `/fundability/` grows by links only. `statements.ts` keeps the twelve statements, their ids and their wording (a fixture in `tests/unit/wp-87/fixtures/readiness-statements.json` pins every field). A separate map, `ALSO_READ`, keyed by statement id, adds up to three further pages per statement. Each is filtered through `liveItems`, so a page that is not live is left out. In the browser a gap shows "Also useful:" with the links; without the script the same links sit in the no-JS hint. `collectGaps` and the gap shape `{id, title, href}` are unchanged, so wp-04, wp-41 and wp-49 pass with no edit. `parseAlsoRead` validates the attribute, drops malformed items and logs invalid JSON.

## Decisions

- Guides take two or more outside sources each and one "About the evidence" section that says which points come from a source and which are working practice. A statute or standards page sources a regulation claim with a quote of 25 words or fewer; vendor documentation (Stripe) sources only a paraphrase.
- Every worked example is invented and carries the exact label "Illustrative example, not a real company." No new image, no HowTo structured data.
- SAFE terms stay inside `safe-explained`. The term sheet guide describes what to ask your counsel and quotes no term as a norm.
- Template bodies carry no internal links, because they are copied out of the site.
- One unquoted `description:` containing a colon and a space broke YAML in ten new files (the unit test for the rubric caught it before any build). All are now double-quoted, and `tests/unit/wp-87/content.test.ts` parses every frontmatter.

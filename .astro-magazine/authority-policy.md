# Authority, sources, rights, and external actions

Record project-specific authority before production:

- approved repository and file scope;
- approved audience, jurisdictions, and risk profile;
- source hierarchy and freshness requirements;
- interview, quote, data, photography, illustration, video, font, component, and generated-media rights;
- privacy, consent, retention, analytics, and form-testing limits;
- whether deployment, publication, redirects, indexing requests, paid services, purchases, accounts, outbound communication, or remote mutations are authorized.

Local editing and public-source research do not imply authority for an external action. Mark absent authority as unknown or blocked; do not infer permission.

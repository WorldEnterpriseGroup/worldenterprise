Founders are often shown funding as a menu, with the amount of money on offer as the only column. This brief reads the same options from the other side and asks what each one requires of the company once the money has arrived.

It covers seven paths: revenue and savings, federal research awards, loans, private sales of shares, convertible instruments, priced rounds and regulated crowdfunding. For each path it reports what a public source says the mechanism is, then separates that report from analysis of which kind of company the path tends to suit.

It also lists questions to put to any path before committing, and it sets out the limits of what its sources can show. Those include rules that change, facts specific to your company and every jurisdiction outside the United States.

The brief is general information for founders. It is not legal, tax or investment advice.

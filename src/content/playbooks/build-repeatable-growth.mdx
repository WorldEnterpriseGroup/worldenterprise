title: Build repeatable growth
description: Define your metrics in writing, run one channel long enough to learn, fix leaks before adding traffic and turn the results into a monthly review.
steps:
  - title: Define your metrics in writing
    body: Write one definition each for monthly recurring revenue, churn, activation and the cost of winning a customer. Use the same definitions in every report so that this month can be compared with last month.
  - title: Pick one channel and run it long enough to learn
    body: Choose the single way of finding customers that has already worked once. Run it for a fixed number of weeks so that you can compare results rather than reacting to one good or bad week.
  - title: Count cost and result for each channel
    body: Each period, record what you spent in money and time, and how many customers you won. Use the written definitions from step one so the figures stay comparable.
  - title: Fix the leaks before adding traffic
    body: Look at onboarding, activation and support before you pour more people in. A leak in the first week costs more than a small gain in reach.
  - title: Write the process down so someone else could run it
    body: Record each step of the channel in plain words, with examples. If it only works when you do it, it is a habit, not a process.
  - title: Review monthly and decide
    body: Once a month, look at the results and choose to put more into a channel, change it or stop. Write the reason down and let the record carry into your investor updates.
artifacts:
  - A metrics definitions sheet
  - A channel log with cost and result for each period
  - A written process for the channel
  - A monthly review with the decision and the reason

## Evidence to check before you move on

Investor readiness starts here. Move to [investor readiness](/fundability/) when you can show each of these.

- The metrics sheet exists, with one definition per measure and a date.
- The channel log covers at least two periods, measured the same way.
- Someone who did not build the channel could follow the written process.
- Monthly reviews record a decision and the reason for it.

These records are what the [investor updates guide](/fundability/investor-updates/) asks you to report from, and what a [seed data room](/fundability/data-room/) holds. Keep them current and they cost almost nothing to share.

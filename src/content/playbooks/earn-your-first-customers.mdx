stage: grow
order: 4
title: Earn your first customers
description: Win your first customers with personal outreach, a scoped pilot, fast onboarding and a conversation log that records why people said yes or no.
steps:
  - title: Name your first ten
    body: List ten specific people or companies by name, drawn from your interviews and from the problem brief. A named list is something you can work through. A market is not.
  - title: Reach out personally, with a specific reason
    body: Write to each one about the problem you heard from them or from people like them. Say what you would like to try together and what you would ask of them.
  - title: Scope a pilot in writing
    body: Agree what the pilot covers, how long it runs and what result would count as success. Write it down with the customer so both sides can check it at the end.
  - title: Shorten the time to first value
    body: Walk each new customer from sign-up to the first result, and note every point where they hesitate. Time to first value is the time between starting and getting something worth having.
  - title: Ask for the commitment
    body: At the end of the pilot ask for what a real customer would give, whether that is payment, a renewal, a referral or a written reference. A clear no teaches you as much as a yes.
  - title: Log every conversation
    body: After each conversation record what they asked for, what they objected to and why they said yes or no. Review the log weekly for patterns and change the product or the offer when the patterns are clear.
artifacts:
  - A named list of first customers
  - A one-page pilot scope that the customer has seen
  - An onboarding checklist
  - A conversation log

## Evidence to check before you move on

Move to [Build repeatable growth](/playbooks/build-repeatable-growth/) when you can show each of these.

- The named list exists, and every name has a status and a date of last contact.
- At least one pilot ran to its written end, and you asked for a commitment at the close.
- The onboarding checklist shows the steps a new customer follows to the first result.
- The conversation log has enough entries that you can point to a pattern and name it.

If the log shows no pattern, go back to your assumptions in [Validate the problem](/playbooks/validate-the-problem/) before you spend on growth.

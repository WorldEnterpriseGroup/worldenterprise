## What the sketch is

A sketch is a conversation starter. It shows the shape of a team: which roles, how many people each, and how the roles group into building the product, building the company and coordinating the work. It lives in this page. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to apply.

## What the sketch is not

It is not a quote, a recommendation or a promise about who will fill a role. WE steers the team and manages the subcontractors who work on it, and the roles are refined when the work is scoped with you. The team scales up or down by notice, so the first sketch does not lock anything in.

## How to use it

Enter a number next to each role you expect to need. Leave the rest empty. The summary updates as you type. When the shape looks right, continue to the application: your roles arrive in the application's team box, ready to edit.

## Where the sketch goes

The sketch is an input to your readiness review, not an order. WE sizes the team you can sustain from where the company stands, and your team schedule in the agreement sets the real roles. [How WE works](/how-we-work/) shows the team WE steers and how it changes from phase to phase.

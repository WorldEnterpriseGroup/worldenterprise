## What the sketch is

A sketch is a conversation starter. It shows the shape of a team: which roles, how many seats each, and how the seats group into building the product, building the company and coordinating the work. It lives in this page. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to apply.

## What the sketch is not

It is not a quote, a recommendation or a promise about who will fill a seat. WE steers the team and manages the subcontractors who fill its seats, and the roles are refined when the work is scoped with you. Seats scale up or down by notice, so the first sketch does not lock anything in.

## How to use it

Enter a number next to each role you expect to need. Leave the rest empty. The summary updates as you type. When the shape looks right, continue to the application: your roles arrive in the application's team box, ready to edit.

## Starting from a package

The pricing page links here with a package, and the sketch names it. You can also choose a company stage yourself, and the application notes the package sized for it. A package comes without preset seats, because this site publishes no seat count for a package. You choose the roles, and WE scopes the plan with you.

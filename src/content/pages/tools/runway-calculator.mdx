## Your numbers stay on this page

The calculator works in your browser. It sends nothing, it saves nothing, and it does not put your numbers in the address bar, so closing the tab removes them. The page makes no request after it loads, and you can confirm that in your browser's network panel.

## What the results are not

The results are arithmetic on the numbers you typed. They are not advice, a forecast, or a statement about whether anyone will invest, and WE makes no promise of funding. The calculator cannot tell whether an entry is right. It does not say how much runway is enough, because that depends on your plan and no source supports a number.

## Level burn and growing burn

Runway at level burn tells you what happens if nothing changes, and something will. It is the same division the <MaybeLink path="/tools/metrics-calculator/">metrics calculator</MaybeLink> uses for runway. The second result lets you try a burn that grows by a percent each month, and the working shows each step so you can check it by hand.

## Round down

Runway is rounded down to one decimal place, because a figure rounded up flatters the position. The <MaybeLink path="/insights/runway-and-burn-the-arithmetic/">article on runway and burn</MaybeLink> works through one invented example.

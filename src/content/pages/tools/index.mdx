Each tool here does one job a founder is asked to do, and each one runs in your browser. You type your own numbers or make your own choices, the page works the result out, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere. None of them needs an account, and none of them quotes a price.

The tools only arrange what you give them. They do not tell you whether a company is ready, they do not predict an outcome, and WE makes no promise of funding. What you do with the result, and what you share, stays with you and your own counsel.

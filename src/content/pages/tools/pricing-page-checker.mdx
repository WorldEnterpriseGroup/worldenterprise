## Your ticks stay on this page

The checker works in your browser. It sends nothing, it saves nothing, and it does not put your ticks in the address bar, so closing the tab removes them. The page makes no request after it loads, and you can confirm that in your browser's network panel.

## What the count is not

The count is not a score, a grade or a verdict on your page. It is arithmetic on the ticks you made, it is not advice, and WE makes no promise of funding. The checker cannot see your page, so it cannot tell whether a tick is true. It checks for presence, not quality.

## Where the statements come from

The statements are the launch checklist at the end of the <MaybeLink path="/insights/pricing-page-fundamentals-for-a-first-saas/">article on pricing page fundamentals</MaybeLink>, in the same order. The article explains why each one matters, and it says to test the page with real readers before launch.

## Read the page as a buyer would

A tick is your own judgment, so the checker is a prompt to look again, not a test the page passes. Ask three people who match your buyer to read the page without your help, and compare what they say with what you ticked.

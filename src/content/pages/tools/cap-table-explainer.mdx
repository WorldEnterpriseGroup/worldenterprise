## Your numbers stay on this page

The explainer works in your browser. It sends nothing, it saves nothing, and it does not put your numbers in the address bar, so closing the tab removes them. The page makes no request after it loads, and you can confirm that in your browser's network panel.

## What the results are not

The results are arithmetic on the numbers you typed. They are not advice, a forecast, or a statement about whether anyone will invest, and WE makes no promise of funding. The explainer cannot tell whether an entry matches your signed documents. It counts shares only, so it cannot tell you what to issue, to whom or on what terms.

## How issuing shares changes ownership

Your ownership is the number of shares you hold divided by all the shares issued. When the company issues new shares to someone else, the number you hold stays the same, but it becomes a smaller part of a larger total, so your ownership falls. That fall is often called dilution. The explainer shows each division and the change, so you can check them by hand.

## What it counts and leaves out

The explainer counts issued shares only. Options, promised options and any other right to shares are left out, because they are not yet shares. A fully diluted count adds them in. The <MaybeLink path="/insights/cap-table-basics-for-founders/">article on cap table basics</MaybeLink> explains what that count contains.

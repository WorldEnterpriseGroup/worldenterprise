## What this checklist is

An evidence room is the organized set of records someone needs to check a company's claims. This checklist joins two guides into one list you can work through: the [seed data room guide](/fundability/data-room/), which names nine folders and what belongs in each, and the [investor readiness checklist](/fundability/investor-readiness-checklist/), which lists twelve statements and the artifact that would show each one is true. The [seed data room checklist](/library/seed-data-room-checklist/) is the same idea as a template to copy.

## How the stages work

The guides do not rank their items by company stage. The stage on each item is WE's own suggestion for the order in which to gather it, so you do not start with the heaviest folders. Choosing a stage shows the items suggested for that stage and every earlier one. It is a suggestion, and no investor is bound to ask for this list or to accept it.

## Where your ticks live

Your ticks and your stage are kept in the part of this page's address after the # sign, and nowhere else. Nothing is stored on your device, and nothing is sent to a server. Bookmark the address, or copy it, to come back to the same list. Anyone who has the address can see what you ticked.

## What the list is not

It is general preparation, not legal or financial advice, and ticking every box does not predict that anyone will invest. WE makes no promise of funding. What you hold, and what you may share, depends on your company and your jurisdiction, so ask your own counsel before a document goes anywhere. Keep passwords, private keys and personal data about customers or staff out of an evidence room.

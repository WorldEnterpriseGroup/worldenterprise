## First, WE reviews the application

WE reviews each application. WE replies to the email address you give. WE does not state a response time on this site.

A clear no is as useful to you as a yes. <MaybeLink path="/program/how-we-decide/">How WE decides</MaybeLink> lists what an application is read for and what WE does not decide.

## Then you and WE agree the scope

If the idea fits, WE and the founder choose which service lines the company needs and what each one will and will not cover. The scope is written down in an agreement, so both companies work from the same page.

WE shares the written agreement before you commit to anything, and you should read it with your own counsel.

## What this page leaves to you

This page repeats only what the program pages already say. Where they are silent, the questions below are for you to put to WE. WE makes no promise of funding.

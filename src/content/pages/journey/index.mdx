The path starts with an application and runs through the same five steps the program pages describe. The pages below take those steps from three angles: what happens after you apply, what you see and decide along the way, and what you can prepare. WE makes no promise of funding.

## Pass it on

If you know a founder who is weighing a program like this one, you can pass this page on. Copy the address from your browser and send it however you usually send a link.

An organization that helps or equips founders may send WE an enquiry through the <MaybeLink path="/network/partners/enquire/">partner enquiry form</MaybeLink>. Sending one creates no partnership, no referral arrangement and no commitment on either side.

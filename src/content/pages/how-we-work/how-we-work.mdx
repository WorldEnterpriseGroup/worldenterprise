Bring WE in the way you would bring in a long-term partner: once, at the start, for as long as the work is useful. WE steers a dedicated, fully remote team that builds the company with you, in phases paced to the years a company really takes.

There is no intake calendar, no class to wait for and no showcase day at the end. The work runs on a written rhythm, every week, month and quarter, with an Orbit Review every year. When the company is ready, WE steps down by notice rather than stopping on a date.

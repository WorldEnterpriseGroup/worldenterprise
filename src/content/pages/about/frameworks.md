WE plans and steers the work with a small set of frameworks that it wrote for its own use. Each one answers a single question: where a company sits, which phase it is in, what a plan has to say, how a task is reported, how a team spends its time. Together they work like a galaxy, and this page draws them that way.

At the core is UBF, the Universal Business Framework. It describes business the way astronomy describes the sky: a project is a droid, a company is a world, a group of companies is a solar system and an industry is a galaxy. The other frameworks are systems inside that map.

These are working methods. They are not industry standards, they have not been independently evaluated, and WE does not claim that using them produces any particular result. Their value is shared language: when someone says what a team is doing, everyone hears the same thing.

## The vocabulary: droids and worlds

WE calls a project a **droid** and a company a **world**. A droid is launched with a single purpose. As it proves itself, it can grow into a world of its own or join one that already exists. Worlds gather into solar systems, and solar systems shape galaxies.

## SuperNova, the journey

The SuperNova strategy strings eight phases together: the D.R.E.A.M. Playbook, the O.R.B.I.T.A.L. Plan, the S.T.A.R.T.U.P. Initiative, A.N.D.R.O.I.D. Ops, the L.A.U.N.C.H. Sequence, the G.R.O.W.T.H. phase, Mission Control and Event Horizon. WE's three stages, Dream, Build and Grow, follow the same path.

## S.T.A.R., for task reporting

A task is first written to be S.M.A.R.T.: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound. Then it is described through four lenses: **Space**, where the task sits and what it depends on; **Track**, where it has been and where it goes next; **Assist**, what is being done today to move it; and **Reach**, what the finished task is expected to change.

## The Focus Shift

WE's framework describes a Focus Shift as a dedicated four-hour block spent on one droid, to enhance or realign its trajectory. A plan for a shift is read against four questions: does it fit the droid's objectives, is it clear, is it feasible in four hours, and what difference could it make.

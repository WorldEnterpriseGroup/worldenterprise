The Universal Business Framework, UBF for short, is how WE describes business: the way astronomy describes the sky. An industry is a galaxy. A holding company and the companies around it form a solar system. A company is a world, and a project that sets out with one purpose is a droid. Seen this way, a new project has neighbors, dependencies and a place on a map.

WE wrote UBF for its own use, and it is the map the other WE frameworks sit on. It is a working method, not an industry standard. It has not been independently evaluated, and WE does not claim that using it produces any particular result. Its value is shared language: when a founder and the team say where a piece of work sits, everyone sees the same picture.

WE makes no promise of funding, and nothing on this page predicts that a company will raise money.

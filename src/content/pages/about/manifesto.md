A manifesto is only worth reading if you can check it against what a company does. These are the commitments WE holds itself to, and where on this site you can test each one.

## 1. Say what WE does, and what it does not

Every service page carries a plain statement of what it does not cover. A promise with no limits is not a promise.

## 2. Promise no funding

WE helps founders prepare evidence for investors. It does not predict or arrange an investment, and it cannot say that anyone will invest.

## 3. Show evidence, not applause

There are no logo walls, no quotes from customers and no performance figures on this site. In their place sit things you can open today: playbooks, templates, sourced briefs and the framework pages. The only counts on the site are counts of what you can open.

## 4. Stay remote by design

WE has no office and asks no one to move. A fully digital company can be honest about how it works because the work is written down.

## 5. Steer the team, and answer for it

A dedicated seat works only on your company. WE steers that team and manages its subcontractors, so there is one accountable party to speak to.

## 6. Cite the source and correct the record

Statements of fact on the site name their source. When WE gets something wrong, the editorial policy says how to tell WE and what happens next.

## 7. Write plainly

Short sentences, concrete nouns, no hype. If a sentence could appear on any company's site, WE rewrites it.

## 8. Leave your counsel your counsel

WE coordinates the professionals around a company: counsel, accountants and specialists. It does not stand in for them and does not give legal or financial advice.

## 9. Keep what WE collects small

The site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts and runs no analytics. A form collects only what its page says, and only when you send it.

## What is World Enterprise Incubator?

World Enterprise Incubator is run by World Enterprise Group Incorporated, a business incubation and consulting firm. WE steers dedicated, fully remote teams that build companies with their founders, paced to the years a company really takes. There are no physical offices.

## What is WE not?

WE makes no promise of funding. WE is a services firm, not an investor. It does not predict or arrange an investment, and it cannot say that anyone will invest.

The program does not give legal, tax or accounting advice, and it does not keep your books or file your taxes. WE does not stand in for a company's counsel or accountants.

There are no logo walls, no quotes from customers and no performance figures on this site.

## What does WE offer?

WE offers seven service lines, A to G, and every one of them states what it does not cover.

- Line A: Brand and Identity
- Line B: Product and IT Development
- Line C: Customer Relations and Market Development
- Line D: Business Management and Operations
- Line E: Digital Incubation
- Line F: Fundability and Investor Readiness
- Line G: Professional-Services Coordination

## How does an engagement work?

It starts with a readiness review: where the company stands, and whether WE is the right help for the years ahead. Each team works on one company at a time. WE's leads (a Delivery Lead, a Technical Lead and an Executive Sponsor) answer to the founder.

WE shares the current terms when you ask. No amounts are published on this site.

## Is Investor Introductions part of the engagement?

No. Investor Introductions is a separate, paid add-on with a flat fee. The fee does not depend on whether any investment closes.

Introductions are a flat-fee service. WE is not a broker-dealer or investment adviser, takes no fee or stake tied to any investment, and cannot promise a meeting or funding.

## How do I reach WE?

Start a readiness review on the Apply page. The contact page takes a question about how WE works, a correction or a press request.

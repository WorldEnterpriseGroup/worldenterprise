## What is World Enterprise Incubator?

World Enterprise Incubator is a digital incubator for SaaS, run by World Enterprise Group Incorporated. Dedicated team seats, steered by WE, take a founder from first idea toward first customers and the evidence an investor will ask for. The work is fully remote. There are no physical offices.

## What is WE not?

WE makes no promise of funding. WE helps founders prepare evidence for investors. It does not predict or arrange an investment, and it cannot say that anyone will invest.

WE describes itself as a digital incubator. It builds with your company through dedicated team seats.

The program does not give legal, tax or accounting advice, and it does not keep your books or file your taxes. WE does not stand in for a company's counsel or accountants.

There are no logo walls, no quotes from customers and no performance figures on this site.

## What does WE offer?

WE offers seven service lines, A to G, and every one of them states what it does not cover.

- Line A: Brand and Identity
- Line B: Product and IT Development
- Line C: Customer Relations and Market Development
- Line D: Business Management and Operations
- Line E: Digital Incubation
- Line F: Fundability and Investor Readiness
- Line G: Professional-Services Coordination

## How do seats and packages work?

The seat is the unit of work. A seat is one dedicated place on your team. A person fills it, works offshore and works only on your company. WE steers the team and manages the subcontractors who fill the seats, so you work with one team instead of coordinating individuals.

Seats can be scaled up or down by notice. The notice mechanics are set out in the written agreement.

A package is a pre-configured seat plan with a fixed scope, sized for a company stage. WE offers four. Every package covers all seven service lines, and the packages differ in depth.

- Spark, depth Core: An idea or a prototype before revenue, with one or two founders and no institutional capital yet.
- Launch, depth Standard: A first product in build or first pilots under way, at the pre-seed stage.
- Accelerate, depth Extended: A working product with early customer evidence, preparing a seed round or an equivalent.
- Studio, depth Embedded: A company with several products, or in a regulated or capital-intensive field, that needs an embedded WE team.

If no package fits, you can build a custom seat plan instead. WE shares the current rate for a package, a custom seat plan or the add-on when you ask, so the figure you receive is the one that applies today. No amounts are published on this site.

## Is Investor Introductions part of a package?

No. Investor Introductions is a separate, paid add-on with a flat fee. The fee does not depend on whether any investment closes.

Introductions are a flat-fee service. WE is not a broker-dealer or investment adviser, takes no fee or stake tied to any investment, and cannot promise a meeting or funding.

## How do I reach WE?

Use the application form on the Apply page. Ask WE a question or apply when you are ready. The contact page also takes a request for the current seat rate or a report of something that needs correcting.

Investors are asked to believe a lot on very little evidence. Readiness means making your claims easy to check: a stated problem, a product people use, numbers you can explain, company records that are in order, and a habit of telling the people who back you what is happening.

None of that decides whether anyone will invest. It only decides whether a serious person can check your story quickly. That is why this section is written as evidence preparation, and why the page begins with the plain statement that WE does not promise funding.

The self-check below lists twelve statements. Tick the ones that are true today and it shows you what is left, with a link to the guide or playbook that helps. It does not score you, and nothing you tick is stored or sent anywhere.

The guides cover the readiness checklist, the data room, how a SAFE works in general terms, a term sheet checklist, answers to security questionnaires, pricing experiments, customer interview synthesis and investor updates. The [library](/library/) holds the matching templates. The guides are general information. For anything specific to your company, ask your own lawyer.

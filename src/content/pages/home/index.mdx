## What WE does, and what it does not do

WE gives a founder a method and a team. It helps you test the idea with real customers, build a first release, and gather the evidence a serious investor asks for. It steers the team that does the work, and it is responsible for how that team is run.

WE does not promise funding, and it does not give legal or tax advice. Your counsel and your accountant stay your own. WE coordinates with them so the company's paperwork keeps pace with its product.

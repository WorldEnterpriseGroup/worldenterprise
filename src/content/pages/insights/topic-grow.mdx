Growth at the earliest stage is not a channel strategy. It is a series of small, checkable commitments from real people: a conversation that confirms a problem, a first session that reaches a result, a price someone agrees to pay, a question answered well enough that the customer stays.

The four articles on this desk follow that order. They cover a founder-led sequence for the first ten customers, onboarding as the final step of acquisition, the fundamentals of a first pricing page and a lightweight way to run support when the customer base is small. Each one gives you a method you can run this week and a record you can open afterwards.

We do not publish benchmarks here. Conversion rates and price points depend on your buyer and your category, and a number borrowed from another company misleads more than it helps. Where an article cites a source, the source is listed so you can read it yourself, and each article says plainly what it does not know.

Taking a first product to market is a run of small decisions made with little evidence: what to say, where to say it, how to charge and how a customer first meets the product. The ten articles on this desk take those decisions in turn. They explain what public rules and documentation say, and they set out questions to put to your own customers, counsel and advisors. They give no benchmark, price or conversion figure. Each ends with a section on what it cannot tell you, and the articles on legal topics describe what a rule is and what to ask a professional.

Being fundable is not a status that someone grants. It is a condition of your records: what you can show, what you own, what you have agreed to and what you can explain. The articles on this desk are about preparing that evidence, and about briefing the lawyers and advisers you choose so they can help you decide.

The five pieces cover what diligence tests, the intellectual property, contract and access records that small engineering teams should keep in order, the questions to bring to your own counsel, the founder time a raise consumes and how to judge investor fit.

Two limits apply throughout. World Enterprise makes no promise of funding, and nothing on this desk predicts that any company will raise money. We also do not give legal or financial advice, and we do not find, approach or introduce investors. Where an article touches the law, it describes general categories from public sources and tells you to ask your own counsel.

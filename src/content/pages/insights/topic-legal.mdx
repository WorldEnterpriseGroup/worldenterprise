The documents a company signs in its first year are hard to change later: the entity filing, the equity split, the assignment of code, the legal pages on the product. The articles on this desk describe what each one is for and what the public sources say about it, so that a founder can bring specific questions to their own lawyer.

The eight articles run from the company outward. They cover choosing an entity, founder equity and vesting, the 83(b) election and its deadline, founder IP assignment and confidentiality, open-source licenses, privacy law, the terms of service and privacy policy, and trademark name clearance.

Two limits apply throughout. World Enterprise is not a law firm, and nothing on this desk is legal or tax advice. Each article carries a dated general-information line, reads primary sources such as statutes and agency pages, and ends with what it cannot tell you.

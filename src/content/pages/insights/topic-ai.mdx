AI-native building means putting a language model inside a product and running it with the risks stated.

One article reads the OWASP list of risks for model applications. Another applies the NIST AI Risk Management Framework to a small team. A third sets out the questions to ask a model provider. A fourth shows how to evaluate a feature before launch. A fifth explains retrieval and where it breaks. A sixth covers metering and budgeting usage. A seventh covers decisions about customer data. An eighth places a person in the loop. The ninth lists questions to bring to counsel about AI regulation.

Each article cites the document it describes and says what it cannot tell you.

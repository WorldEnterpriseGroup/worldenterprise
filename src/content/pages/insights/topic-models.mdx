Models is the desk for choosing how a software company makes money and reaches customers. The choice shapes what you build first, whom you hire and how you talk to buyers.

The eight articles on this desk describe the main models in plain terms: business-to-business SaaS, vertical SaaS, marketplaces, platforms and ecosystems, seat, usage and hybrid pricing, product-led and sales-led growth, API-first products and open-core businesses. Each one says what the model asks of a first company and what it cannot tell you. None gives benchmarks.

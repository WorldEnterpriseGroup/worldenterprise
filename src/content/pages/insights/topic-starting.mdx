Starting out is the stage before the idea has a company around it. The questions here are about the founder: what you bring, what is missing and what to settle before the work begins. The mistakes made at this stage are quiet ones, because nothing has been built yet to show them.

Eight articles on this desk are written for different starting points. They cover first-time, technical, non-technical, solo, student, second-career and corporate founders, and founders working from outside the United States. Begin with the one closest to your situation. Then read the two decision articles, on choosing a co-founder and on moving from part-time to full-time. Each piece names its public sources and says what it cannot tell you.

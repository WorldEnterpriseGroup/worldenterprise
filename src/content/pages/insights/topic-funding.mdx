Funding is the desk for understanding the money itself: the routes open to a software company, the instruments investors use, the rules around them and how long cash lasts. It sits beside the Fundable desk, which is about preparing your evidence.

The ten articles cover routes from customer revenue to grants, loans and crowdfunding, how a SAFE differs from a convertible note, how to read a term sheet, how a cap table records ownership, and how to work out burn and runway. Each says where its sources stop and lists questions for your own counsel or accountant.

Two limits apply throughout. World Enterprise makes no promise of funding, and nothing here predicts that any company will raise money. World Enterprise gives no legal or financial advice. An article says what a document or rule is, and what to ask a professional you choose.

Hiring is a run of decisions, and most are made before anyone is interviewed.

Nine articles follow the path. One lists what to settle before you write the job. Another sets out the tests agencies use to tell a contractor from an employee. Another shows how to write a job description a candidate can answer. Another describes a structured interview at small scale. Another covers what an offer letter should state. Another sets out the questions to ask before hiring in another country. Another explains what an option is and what to ask about one. Another covers writing down how a remote team works. The last covers what to prepare before a new person's first day.

Legal and tax articles are general information, not advice, and each names its public sources.

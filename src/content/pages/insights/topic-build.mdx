Build is the blueprint stage: turning a validated problem into a first release with a team you may never meet in person. The failure modes are well known and mostly self-inflicted. Scope grows because nobody drew a cut line. Architecture is decided early for a scale the company has not reached. Remote work degrades into a stream of unanswered messages. Components that were never the product, such as sign-in, billing and email, absorb months.

The articles on this desk deal with each of those in turn, and with the habits a first release needs: product decisions, requirements, estimates in ranges, a plain first stack, flags with a way back, and a view of what you shipped. Each names its public sources and says plainly where the evidence stops.

Companies begin in different places: an idea, a skill, a client, an employer, a product someone else built or a side project.

The articles on this desk set those starting points, and the ways to pay for the work that follows, side by side. None of them ranks the routes or tells you which one to take.

Several cover law and tax, so each carries a dated note that it is general information, not legal or financial advice, and lists questions to bring to a professional licensed where you operate. Each article cites its sources so you can read them yourself, and says plainly what it cannot tell you.

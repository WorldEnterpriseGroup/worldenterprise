Dream is the survey stage: the work you do before a team is hired and before a line of code is written. The mistakes made here are the most expensive ones, because they are invisible. A founder who skips the problem brief, trusts friendly interview answers or sizes a market from a top-down report can spend a year building something nobody asked for and never see the warning.

The four articles on this desk are practical and ordered. Start by asking whether the idea is a business or only a feature. Learn to interview customers without collecting false encouragement. Size the market from the bottom up, using counts you can verify. Then write the problem brief, so that everything built afterwards can be checked against it. Each piece names its public sources and says what it cannot tell you.

Running a company takes upkeep alongside building it, and the founder is part of that system.

Eight articles follow. One maps the kinds of obligation on a US tax calendar. Another explains what the books are for and what the IRS does and does not prescribe. Another lists the kinds of business insurance a US agency describes and what to ask an agent. Another gives criteria for choosing the tools a company runs on. Another sets out a weekly rhythm for a founder. Another covers meetings, written work and decision records. Another describes a process for a co-founder disagreement and what mediation is. The last covers burnout and where public bodies point for help.

Tax, insurance and health articles are general information, not advice, and each names its public sources.

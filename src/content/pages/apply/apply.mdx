Tell WE about the idea. The form has four short steps: who you are, where the company stands, the team and horizon you have in mind, and a review before you send. Only your name, your email address and the company's stage are required. Without JavaScript the core questions appear on one page.

WE is fully remote, so there is nothing to book and nowhere to travel. You apply from where you are.

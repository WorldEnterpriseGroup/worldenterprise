Tell WE about the idea. The form has four short steps: who you are, where the idea stands, the team and package you have in mind, and a review before you send. The team and the package are optional, and so is a longer description of what you are building. Without JavaScript the same questions appear on one page.

WE is fully remote, so there is nothing to book and nowhere to travel. You apply from where you are.

A package is a pre-configured seat plan with a fixed scope, sized for a company stage. WE sets the seat plan inside each package to match its depth, and confirms it in writing when you ask for the current rate.

This page lists no amounts. WE shares the current rate for a package, a custom seat plan or the add-on when you ask, so the figure you receive is the one that applies today. Ask on the <a href={href('/contact/?topic=seat-rate')}>contact form</a>. The page lists no seat counts either.

If no package fits, you can build a custom seat plan instead. Choose the roles you need and WE scopes the plan with you. The <a href={href('/tools/team-composer/')}>Team Composer</a> turns your choices into a sketch you can share.

## What drives the number of seats

The number of seats follows the work in front of you. Four things shape it:

- **The stage of the idea.** Validating a problem with customers needs a different team from shipping a first release.
- **The roles the product needs.** A product with a complex back end asks for different seats from a brand-led product.
- **The work in the current cycle.** Seats can grow when there is more to build and shrink when there is less.
- **How much you can review and decide each week.** A team moves at the speed of its decisions, so the founder's time is part of the plan.

## Questions about seats and packages

<div class="faq-list">
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Is there a price list?">
<p>Not on this page. WE shares the current rate for a package, a custom seat plan or the add-on when you ask for it.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="What is a package?">
<p>A package is a pre-configured seat plan with a fixed scope, sized for a company stage. WE offers Spark, Launch, Accelerate and Studio. Each covers all seven service lines at a different depth.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="How do I find the package for my stage?">
<p>Each package names the company stage it is sized for. Answer the stage question on this page and it points to the matching package, or read the four stage descriptions and pick the one that matches your company.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="What does depth mean?">
<p>Every package covers all seven service lines, A to G. Depth is how far WE goes in each of them. It runs from Core in Spark, through Standard in Launch and Extended in Accelerate, to Embedded in Studio.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Can I use a custom seat plan instead of a package?">
<p>Yes. Choose the roles you need and WE scopes the plan with you. The Team Composer helps you sketch one.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Can I change the number of seats later?">
<p>Yes. In a package or a custom plan, seats scale up or down by notice. The written agreement sets out how.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Is Investor Introductions part of a package?">
<p>No. It is a separate, paid add-on with a flat fee. The fee does not depend on whether any investment closes.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Who manages the people in a seat?">
<p>WE does. WE steers the team and manages its subcontractors.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Do I need an account to use the Team Composer?">
<p>No. It needs no sign-up, shows no price and keeps nothing. Your sketch lives in the page, and in the link if you choose to carry it to the application.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Does WE promise that my company will raise money?">
<p>No. WE makes no promise of funding. The program helps you build evidence that a serious investor looks for.</p>
</Faq>
<Faq name="pricing-faq" question="Where do I start?">
<p>Choose a package above, or sketch a team in the Team Composer, or go straight to the application and describe the idea.</p>
</Faq>

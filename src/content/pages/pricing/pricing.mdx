WE no longer publishes packages or a price list. Every company is paced to its own business clock, so the terms are set in the agreement you review with your own counsel, not on a public page.

The page that replaces this one, How WE works, sets out the steps before Day One, the five phases, the weekly to yearly rhythm, how WE is paid and how the work ends.

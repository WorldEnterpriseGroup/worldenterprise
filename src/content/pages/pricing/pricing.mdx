## How seats work

A seat is one dedicated place on your team. A person fills it, works offshore and works only on your company. You choose the roles you need. WE steers the team and manages the subcontractors who fill the seats, so you work with one team instead of coordinating individuals.

The seat is the unit of work. Packages and custom seat plans are two ways to arrange seats. You can scale up or down by notice, whichever you choose. The notice terms are set out in the written agreement, not on this page.

## Choose a package or a custom seat plan

A package is a pre-configured seat plan with a fixed scope, sized for a company stage. WE offers four. Every package covers all seven service lines, A to G, and the packages differ in depth.

WE sets the seat plan inside each package to match its depth, and confirms it in writing when you ask for the current rate. This page lists no seat counts.

If no package fits, you can build a custom seat plan instead. Choose the roles you need and WE scopes the plan with you. The <a href={href('/tools/team-composer/')}>Team Composer</a> turns your choices into a sketch you can share.

## What drives the number of seats

The number of seats follows the work in front of you. Four things shape it:

- **The stage of the idea.** Validating a problem with customers needs a different team from shipping a first release.
- **The roles the product needs.** A product with a complex back end asks for different seats from a brand-led product.
- **The work in the current cycle.** Seats can grow when there is more to build and shrink when there is less.
- **How much you can review and decide each week.** A team moves at the speed of its decisions, so the founder's time is part of the plan.

## What this page does not show

This page lists no amounts. WE shares the current rate for a package, a custom seat plan or the add-on when you ask, so the figure you receive is the one that applies today. Ask on the <a href={href('/contact/?topic=seat-rate')}>contact form</a>.

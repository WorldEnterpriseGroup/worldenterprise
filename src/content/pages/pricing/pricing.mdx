## How seats work

A seat is one dedicated place on your team. A person fills it, works offshore and works only on your company. You choose the roles you need. WE steers the team and manages the subcontractors who fill the seats, so you work with one accountable team instead of coordinating individuals.

You can scale up or down by notice. The notice terms are set out in the written agreement, not on this page.

## What drives the number of seats

The number of seats follows the work in front of you, not a fixed package. Four things shape it:

- **The stage of the idea.** Validating a problem with customers needs a different team from shipping a first release.
- **The roles the product needs.** A product with a complex back end asks for different seats from a brand-led product.
- **The work in the current cycle.** Seats can grow when there is more to build and shrink when there is less.
- **How much you can review and decide each week.** A team moves at the speed of its decisions, so the founder's time is part of the plan.

The <a href={href('/tools/team-composer/')}>Team Composer</a> turns these into a sketch you can share.

## What this page does not show

This page does not list a price, a package or a fee schedule. WE shares the current seat rate when you ask, so the figure you receive is the one that applies today. Ask on the <a href={href('/contact/?topic=seat-rate')}>contact form</a>.

WE does not publish a list of technical controls on this page, and it does not describe a founder's product as secure. What it does is put security and confidentiality terms in the written agreement for an engagement, where both sides can read them and hold each other to them.

Before you start a readiness review, ask for those terms. Read them against your own needs. If something you need is missing, say so and have your own lawyer check the result.

The same discipline applies to this website. The facts above are things you can check yourself in your browser, and the privacy notice says what each form collects.

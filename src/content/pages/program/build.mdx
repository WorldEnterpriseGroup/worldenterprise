Build covers two phases. **Build** is the first release, the first customers and the first hires, run on the S.T.A.R.T.U.P. Initiative and A.N.D.R.O.I.D. Ops. **Launch** makes sales repeatable and finds product-market fit, run on the L.A.U.N.C.H. Sequence.

Build starts from a problem you have tested. From the blueprint altitude, the team draws what a first release must do, cuts the scope to that, and builds it in reviewed cycles. A dedicated team does the work, and WE steers it. The aim is a first product that a real customer can use, plus the paper trail that explains it: the scope, the decisions and the open items.

You keep a security baseline and a first release, a customer evidence index and a financial model. The first year is the steepest on the clock, so the year-one Orbit Review sits right where the data shows most companies close.

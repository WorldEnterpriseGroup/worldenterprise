Build starts from a problem you have tested. From the blueprint altitude, the team draws what a first release must do, cuts the scope to that, and builds it in reviewed cycles. Dedicated seats do the work and WE steers them.

The aim is a first product that a real customer can use, plus the paper trail that explains it: the scope, the decisions and the open items. Nothing in this stage depends on guessing what customers want, because Dream has already asked them.

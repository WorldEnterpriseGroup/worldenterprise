After Day One, the team works through the stages at the pace the company needs: Dream for the first proof, Build through the steepest year and Grow toward an established company. Each stage ends with things the company keeps, and a phase checkpoint where you decide what comes next.

A company that is already operating starts at the stage it is in. The readiness review places it.

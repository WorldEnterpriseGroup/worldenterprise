Dream is where a SaaS idea meets its first real test. The team looks at the problem from the survey altitude: wide enough to see who has it, how they cope today and whether anyone would change what they do. Nothing is built yet, and that is the point.

Most of the work is conversation and writing. You and WE agree who to talk to, run the interviews, and turn what you hear into a short brief that anyone can read. At the end you have a decision, and the evidence for it.

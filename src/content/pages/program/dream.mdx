In Dream, the team works through the D.R.E.A.M. Playbook (Discover, Relate, Empower, Advise, Motivate) and writes the O.R.B.I.T.A.L. Plan (Objective, Resources, Budget, Indicators, Transport, Activities, Logistics).

You leave the stage with a readiness scorecard and plan, the ownership records in order and a brand that says what the company does. WE judges the stage by what you can open and check, not by how much time has passed.

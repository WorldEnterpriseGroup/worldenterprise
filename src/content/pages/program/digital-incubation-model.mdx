WE has no physical offices. That is a design choice, not a gap. A team that is built to work remotely writes things down, makes decisions where others can read them, and does not depend on who happened to be in the room.

For a founder it means two practical things. You do not relocate, and you do not wait for a meeting to find out where your product stands. The state of the work lives in documents and reviews that you can open at any time.

The team is part of the engagement. The team WE steers works on your company and on nothing else. You decide where the company is going. WE decides how the team gets there with you, and answers for how it is run.

You do not recruit, contract or manage the people on the team. WE does, including any subcontractors it brings in. You review the work and set the direction.

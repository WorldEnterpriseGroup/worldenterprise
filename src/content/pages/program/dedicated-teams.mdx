A seat is one dedicated place on your company's team. It is filled by an offshore specialist who works on your company and on nothing else. You decide where the company is going. WE decides how the team gets there with you, and answers for how it is run.

You do not recruit, contract or manage the people in the seats. WE does, including any subcontractors it brings in. You review the work and set the direction.

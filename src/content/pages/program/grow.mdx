Grow puts the product in front of customers. From the territory altitude you can see what is happening on the ground: who signed up, who stayed, who left and why. The work is to earn customers one at a time, then to make that work repeatable.

Grow is also where the evidence for investors gets assembled: definitions, a data room, regular updates. These help any company be understood, whether or not it ever raises money. The program makes no promise of funding.

WE is a long-horizon business incubation and consulting firm. A founder brings an idea or an early product. WE brings a method, a way of steering a team, and a dedicated team that WE steers, working only on that company. WE stays for as long as the work is useful.

The program is fully remote, so nobody relocates and there is no office to visit. Roles on the team change by notice, and WE manages the team and any subcontractors on the founder's behalf.

The work runs in three stages and five phases, paced to the clock new businesses actually follow: the first proof and the plan, the first release and the first customers, repeatable sales, and the years of becoming established. Each phase closes at a checkpoint, and every week the founder sees the status in writing and says yes or no to the next cycle's plan.

WE is a digital incubator for SaaS. A founder brings an idea or an early product. WE brings a method, a way of steering a team, and dedicated team seats that work only on that company.

The program is fully remote, so nobody relocates and there is no office to visit. The seats are offshore, they scale up or down by notice, and WE manages the team and any subcontractors on the founder's behalf.

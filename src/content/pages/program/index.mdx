WE is a business incubation and consulting firm. WE steers a dedicated, fully remote team that builds a company with its founder, for as long as the work is useful.

The work runs in three stages and five phases, paced to the clock new businesses actually follow: the first proof and the plan, the first release and the first customers, repeatable sales, and the years of becoming established. Each phase closes at a checkpoint, and every week the founder sees the status in writing and says yes or no to the next cycle's plan.

## Founders who mean to build a lasting company

WE works with founders who mean to build a company that lasts, especially those whose experience predicts success, and capable founders who need more capacity than they can hire. This page describes the working relationship in plain terms so you can decide before you start a readiness review. It publishes no founder stories and shows no client list.

The criteria below are how WE decides whether a company is a fit. They are about the business and the founder's habits. They are not about who you know or where you studied.

## What WE asks of a founder

Three things matter more than the rest. A founder keeps making the decisions about the product and the company. A founder speaks with customers often and shares what was learned. A founder reads the written plan for each checkpoint and says yes or no to it.

The team WE steers works on one company at a time. It cannot replace the founder's judgment about the customer, and it is not meant to.

## What a founder keeps doing

You own the company, its decisions and its customer relationships. You hire your own lawyer and accountant, and WE coordinates with them when you ask it to. You decide when the product is ready to show customers. You decide whether and when to raise money, and from whom.

## Founders, described by what they bring

WE builds software companies with founders who already know whose problem they want to solve. This page describes the working relationship in plain terms so you can decide before you apply. This page publishes no founder stories and shows no portfolio.

The criteria below are how WE decides whether a company is a fit. They are about the idea and the founder's habits. They are not about who you know or where you studied.

## What WE asks of a founder

Three things matter more than the rest. A founder keeps making the decisions about the product and the company. A founder speaks with customers every week, in person or on a call, and shares what was learned. A founder reads the written plan for each cycle and says yes or no to it.

The team WE seats works only on your company. It cannot replace the founder's judgement about the customer, and it is not meant to.

## What a founder keeps doing

You own the company, its decisions and its customer relationships. You hire your own lawyer and accountant, and WE coordinates with them when you ask it to. You decide when the product is ready to show customers. You decide whether and when to raise money, and from whom.

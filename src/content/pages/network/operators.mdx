## Seats are filled by roles

A seat is a place on a dedicated team that works only on your company. Each seat holds one role. You choose the roles your company needs, and you can scale the team up or down by notice as the work changes.

WE steers the team and manages any subcontractors it uses. You deal with one team and not with a chain of vendors. The team is fully remote.

## Roles, not individuals

This page lists functions. It does not name the people or firms who fill them, and it does not describe any person's background. A role description tells you what to expect from the function and where it stops, so you can plan the rest.

The role list is the same list the Team Composer uses, so what you sketch there matches what is described here.

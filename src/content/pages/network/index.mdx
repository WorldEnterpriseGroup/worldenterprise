## How the pieces fit

A founder applies. WE scopes the work with the founder and seats a dedicated team for that company. The team is made of defined roles, WE steers it, and WE manages any subcontractors it uses. As the work produces evidence, WE helps the founder organise that evidence so a serious investor can read it.

Each step has its own page. The founder page says what WE asks of you and what you keep doing. The operators page lists the roles that can staff a seat. The investors page says what WE does for companies preparing to raise, and what it does not do.

## The standards WE works to

WE steers a team with a small set of documented operating frameworks. Those are described on the frameworks page. The public standards WE maps its work to are named on the trust center. Naming a standard is a statement about what WE reads and works from. It is not a claim of certification for WE or for any company.

## What the network is not

There is no logo wall here, because there is no client list to show. There is no investor network, because WE does not offer one. There is no partner programme or perks scheme. If any of that changes, it will appear on its own page with the names of people and companies who agreed in writing to be named.

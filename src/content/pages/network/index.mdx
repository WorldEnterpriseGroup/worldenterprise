## How the pieces fit

A founder applies. WE scopes the work with the founder and seats a dedicated team for that company. The team is made of defined roles, WE steers it, and WE manages any subcontractors it uses. As the work produces evidence, WE helps the founder organize that evidence so a serious investor can read it.

Each step has its own page. The founder page says what WE asks of you and what you keep doing. The operators page lists the roles that can staff a seat. The investors page says what WE does for companies preparing to raise, and what it does not do. The partners page says how an organization that helps founders can send WE an enquiry.

## Partners

An organization that helps or equips founders may send WE an enquiry. The partners page lists six categories, from tooling and software products to ecosystem and community programs. An enquiry creates no partnership, no referral arrangement and no commitment on either side. WE names no partner on this site until a written record exists.

## The standards WE works to

WE steers a team with a small set of documented operating frameworks. Those are described on the frameworks page. The public standards WE maps its work to are named on the trust center. Naming a standard is a statement about what WE reads and works from. It is not a claim of certification for WE or for any company.

## What the network is not

There is no logo wall, no client list and no investor network. If that changes, it will appear on its own page with the names of people and companies who agreed in writing to be named. Investor Introductions is a paid add-on for one company at a time. It is not a membership of any network.

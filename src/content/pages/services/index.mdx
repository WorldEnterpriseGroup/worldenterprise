## What every line has in common

Each line has the same shape. It says what it covers, what it does not do, what you will hold at the end and which playbooks sit behind it. The edges are written down on purpose. A company that knows what a service does not do can plan for the rest.

One dedicated team carries the work. WE steers that team and manages any subcontractors it uses, so you have one point of contact and not a chain of vendors. The team works fully remotely, and it can be scaled up or down by notice as your needs change.

No line promises funding. Several of them help you gather the evidence that investors ask for, and that is where WE's work stops.

The playbooks follow the order in which a SaaS company is usually built: validate the problem, design the product, build and ship version one, earn the first customers, and build growth that repeats. They sit across the three stages WE works in: Dream, Build and Grow.

Each playbook is a short sequence of steps. Every step names the thing you should have in hand when it is done, so you can tell the difference between finishing a step and merely talking about it. Each playbook also lists the mistakes that cost founders the most time, and an evidence check to run before you move on.

You do not need to read them in order, but the later ones assume the earlier work exists. If you cannot yet write down who has the problem and what they do about it today, start with the first playbook, not the fourth.

The playbooks are general working guides. They are not a promise about how any company will perform, and they do not replace advice from your own lawyer, accountant or other qualified adviser.

## Read this first

WE is a digital incubator for SaaS. It prepares a company's evidence. It is not a fund, a broker, an adviser or a marketplace, and this page is written to make that plain to anyone who arrives from an investor's side.

## What WE does

For companies that use its service lines, WE helps the founder gather what a serious investor asks for: a clear problem and customer, a record of what was tried, a product that works, a readable set of numbers the company defines itself, and a data room that is organized and kept current. WE also helps a founder write regular investor updates in a consistent shape.

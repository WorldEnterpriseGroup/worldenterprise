This page sets two categories of support side by side on the same questions. Each statement about a category names a public source, and the sources are listed at the end of the page.

## Where WE sits

WE is a digital incubator for SaaS companies: dedicated offshore team seats steered by WE, fully remote, with no promise of funding.

## WE is probably not the right fit if

- Your idea is not a software-as-a-service product.
- You want WE to run your product in production or to provide round-the-clock support.

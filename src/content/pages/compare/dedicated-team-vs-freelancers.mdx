This page sets two categories of support side by side on the same questions. Each statement about a category names a public source, and the sources are listed at the end of the page.

## Where WE sits

A seat is not a freelancer you must recruit, brief and manage. WE does that.

You do not recruit, contract or manage the people in the seats. WE does, including any subcontractors it brings in.

## WE is probably not the right fit if

- You want WE to give legal or tax advice, keep your books or file your taxes.

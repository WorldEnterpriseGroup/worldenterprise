## Where WE sits

The program is fully remote, so nobody relocates and there is no office to visit. Roles on the team change by notice, and WE manages the team and any subcontractors on the founder's behalf.

## WE is probably not the right fit if

- You need a physical office, on-site staff or in-person work. WE is fully remote.

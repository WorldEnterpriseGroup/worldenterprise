This page sets two categories of support side by side on the same questions. Each statement about a category names a public source, and the sources are listed at the end of the page.

## Where WE sits

WE is a long-horizon business incubation and consulting firm. A founder brings an idea or an early product. WE brings a method, a way of steering a team, and a dedicated team that WE steers, working only on that company.

The program is fully remote, so nobody relocates and there is no office to visit.

## WE is probably not the right fit if

- You need a physical office, on-site staff or in-person work. WE is fully remote.
- You are looking for money rather than a team. WE makes no promise of funding.

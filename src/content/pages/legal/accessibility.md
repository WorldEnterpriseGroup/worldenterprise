World Enterprise Group Incorporated wants everyone to be able to read this site and use its forms. The site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That is a target. WE has not had the site independently audited and does not claim that it conforms in every respect.

## What the site does

- Every page has a link at the top that skips to the main content, one main landmark, one level-one heading and headings that do not skip levels.
- Navigation works with a keyboard alone, and the menus open and close with the keyboard. The navigation also works with scripts switched off, because each top-level item is a plain link to a page that lists its sections.
- Focus is visible on every interactive element, and controls are sized for touch.
- Text and interface colors are checked against the contrast minimums for their size, and no information is carried by color alone.
- Motion is limited, and it is removed when your system asks for reduced motion.
- Every informative image has a text description, and decorative images are marked so that assistive technology skips them.
- Forms use visible labels, say what is required, and list errors in a summary that links to each field.
- Pages reflow at narrow widths and at 200 percent zoom without sideways scrolling.

## How the site is tested

Automated accessibility checks run against every page each time the site is built, and a build with a failing check does not ship. Automated tools find only part of the problems, which is why the list of known gaps below includes manual testing.

## Known gaps

This list is kept honest. It will change as gaps are fixed.

- No independent accessibility audit has been completed. Manual testing with a range of screen readers and magnifiers is still to be done.
- Illustrative images are created or generated for this site, and their descriptions are written from the intended picture rather than checked by a person who is blind or has low vision.
- Forms depend on an intake service that is separate from this site. If that service is unavailable the form shows a message and keeps your answers on the page; a mail link is offered only when WE has a monitored mailbox for it.
- Printed versions of briefs and templates are provided through the browser's print function and have not been tested with every printer driver or reader.

## Tell WE about a barrier

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, use the contact page and choose the topic "A general question". Say which page, what you were trying to do, and what technology you use, if you are willing to share it. WE reviews each report and treats a barrier as a defect to fix. WE does not promise a response time.

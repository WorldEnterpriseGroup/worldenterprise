This notice describes what happens to information when you use worldenterprise.org. World Enterprise Group Incorporated ("WE"), a company registered in Delaware, is responsible for the information you send through this site.

## Browsing the site

This site sets no cookies. It loads no third-party scripts, no analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels and no fonts or images from other companies. WE does not run a server that logs your visits.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical data about each request, such as your network address and browser details, and handles it under its own terms and privacy statement. WE does not receive that data from GitHub.

## What a form collects

Nothing is collected until you press the submit button of a form. Nothing is sent while you type. The forms on this site are the Apply form, the Contact form and, where it is switched on, the newsletter form. A form sends only the fields below.

| Field | Sent as | Which forms | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which form it is | `form` | all | So WE can route the message |
| Your name | `name` | Apply, Contact | To address you |
| Your email address | `email` | all | To reply to you or send the newsletter |
| Company or working name | `company` | Apply, Contact | Optional context |
| Stage of your idea | `stage` | Apply | To understand where you are |
| Team seats of interest | `seats` | Apply | Optional text, filled by the Team Composer if you use it |
| Topic | `topic` | Contact | To route the message |
| Your message | `message` | Apply (optional), Contact | The substance of what you want to say |
| Your consent | `consent` | all | You tick a box that links to this notice |
| Hidden spam trap | `website` | all | A field people leave empty; automated senders fill it |
| Time you started and sent | `startedAt`, `submittedAt` | all | A weak signal to spot automated senders |
| Page it was sent from | `page` | all | So WE knows which form it was |

The newsletter form uses only `email`, `consent`, the spam trap, the two times and the page.

## Where it goes

A form posts your answers over an encrypted connection to an intake service that is separate from this website. The service passes each submission to a monitored mailbox or case system that WE names. The form sends no cookies and does not tell the service which page you came from beyond the `page` field above. Where the intake service is not yet connected, the form says so and sends nothing.

WE uses what you send to answer you, to consider your application, to send you the newsletter if you asked for it, and to keep a record of the conversation. WE does not sell it and does not pass it to advertisers.

## Spam protection

The spam trap and the time check are the only controls used against automated senders. There is no CAPTCHA and no third-party bot-detection service.

## The Team Composer

The Team Composer runs in your browser. It stores nothing, sets no cookies and sends nothing. If you choose to continue to the Apply form, the roles you picked travel in the link you follow, in the part after the hash sign, which your browser does not send to any server.

## Your choices

You can ask what WE holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Use the contact page and choose the topic about how WE handles information. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

## Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, for example public standards and the WE organisation on GitHub. Those sites have their own practices, and this notice does not cover them.

## Changes to this notice

If the site changes what it collects, this notice changes first and its date at the top is updated.

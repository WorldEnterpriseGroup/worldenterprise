Executive briefs are longer written documents for people who need to decide something and want the considerations laid out in one place. Each brief has its own page with an outline of what it covers. WE sends the full text by email to the address you give when you request it.

Every brief names its sources, states how far the evidence goes and says what it cannot tell you. A brief is general information for founders. It is not legal, tax or investment advice.

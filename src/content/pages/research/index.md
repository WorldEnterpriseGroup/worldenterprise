These briefs are short reports for founders who need a defensible answer to a practical question. Each one states its method, names what it could not verify, lists its sources, and ends with an appendix you can use: a checklist, a decision matrix or a formula table. Each prints cleanly.

## How the briefs are made

Three of the four briefs report what public primary documents state, such as the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard, the NIST Secure Software Development Framework, the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines and the US Department of Justice web rule. The staffing brief is analysis and says so. The metrics brief carries formulas, never benchmarks.

Every factual statement carries a reference to a numbered source, and every brief is dated "as of" the day its sources were read, so you can judge how current it is. Where a source could not be read in full, the brief says so under Limitations and leaves the detail out rather than guessing.

## What the briefs are not

They are not legal, audit or compliance advice, and they do not describe controls operated by World Enterprise Group Incorporated or any other company. They contain no client results.

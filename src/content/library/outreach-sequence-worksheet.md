## 2. The sequence

Plan every message before you send the first. A sequence is short, and each message adds something new rather than repeating the last.

## 3. Each message must

- \[ \] Come from a sender the recipient can identify, using your real name and company.
- \[ \] Have a subject line that matches what the message says.
- \[ \] State the reason you are writing in the first sentence.
- \[ \] Make one ask that can be answered in a sentence.
- \[ \] Say how the recipient can stop hearing from you, and make that easy.
- \[ \] Include whatever contact or address information the rules in your location require. Ask your counsel what applies to commercial messages where you and the recipient are based.

## 7. Keep it honest

- \[ \] Nothing in any message is untrue or exaggerated.
- \[ \] You did not claim a connection, a customer or a partner that does not exist.
- \[ \] You can explain where every contact came from.
- \[ \] You stopped when asked.

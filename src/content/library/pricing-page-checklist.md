## 1. Clarity

- \[ \] Each plan says who it is for, in one sentence.
- \[ \] Each plan lists what is included, in the same order as the other plans.
- \[ \] The page says what costs extra, or says that nothing does.
- \[ \] The billing term is stated: monthly, annual or both.
- \[ \] The unit you charge for is stated: per seat, per account, per use or flat.
- \[ \] The page says how a customer changes or cancels a plan.

## 2. Honesty

- \[ \] The price is shown, or the page says plainly how a visitor gets a quote.
- \[ \] Every fee is shown before checkout, not first at checkout.
- \[ \] Any trial states exactly what it includes, when it ends and what happens at the end.
- \[ \] Any discount states its conditions on the same screen.
- \[ \] Taxes are described accurately for the places you sell to. Ask your counsel or accountant what applies.
- \[ \] The page does not describe a plan as popular, best or most chosen unless you can show it.

## 3. Comparison

- \[ \] The differences between plans are in one table.
- \[ \] The table uses words as well as symbols, so it makes sense to a screen reader.
- \[ \] A visitor can tell which plan fits without contacting you.
- \[ \] The table does not rely on color alone to show what is included.

## 4. Trust

- \[ \] The terms of service and the privacy notice are linked from the page.
- \[ \] The cancellation and refund policy is linked, and matches what you actually do.
- \[ \] The page says where to get help before buying.
- \[ \] No logo, quote or number appears without permission and a source you can show.

## 5. Accessibility

- \[ \] Text has enough contrast against its background.
- \[ \] Every control can be reached and used with a keyboard.
- \[ \] The page works when zoomed to twice its size without sideways scrolling.
- \[ \] Prices and plan names are text, not images.

## 6. Price change log

Never overwrite a price. Add a row each time a price, a package or a billing term changes, and keep the old row.

## 7. Before you publish

- \[ \] You wrote down what you expect to happen, and what you will do if it does not.
- \[ \] Existing customers know what changes for them, and when.
- \[ \] The change is recorded in your decision log.
- \[ \] Your counsel has seen any wording about renewals, price changes or refunds.

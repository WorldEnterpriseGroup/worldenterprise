## 1. The visitor

- **Who arrives on this page:** the role and the situation, in one sentence.
- **How they got here:** the search, message, referral or link that sent them.
- **What they already know:** what they have heard about the problem and about your product.
- **What they are comparing you with:** the alternative they use today, including doing nothing.

## 2. The one action

- **The single action you want:** one verb and one object, for example "book a call" or "join the waiting list".
- **What the visitor gets when they take it:** state it exactly, with no surprises.
- **What it costs the visitor:** time, information, money or commitment. Say so on the page.

## 7. Claims check

- \[ \] Every sentence on the page is true today.
- \[ \] Every number on the page can be shown to a reader who asks.
- \[ \] No claim compares you with a named competitor unless you can support it.
- \[ \] The page says what happens after the visitor takes the action.
- \[ \] The page can be read and used with a keyboard and a screen reader.
- \[ \] Any form says what you will do with the information it collects.

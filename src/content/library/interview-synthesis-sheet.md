## 8. Checks before you share the sheet

- \[ \] Every finding points to specific interviews.
- \[ \] Predictions are not counted as behavior.
- \[ \] Disagreeing interviews are listed.
- \[ \] Names and contact details are not on the sheet.
- \[ \] Contact details are deleted when you no longer need them.
- \[ \] The sheet is saved with the date, next to your conversation log.

## 1. The problem in one sentence

Write the problem as the customer would say it. Do not mention your solution.

> Example shape: "When [situation], [type of person] struggles to [task] because [cause]."

## 2. Who has it

- **Primary customer:** the role and the kind of organization, in one line.
- **Who pays:** the person or budget that would pay, if different from the user.
- **Who else is affected:** people who use, approve or block the work.

## 3. What they do today

- The current workaround, tool or process.
- What it costs them in time, money or risk, described in their words.
- Why the workaround is not good enough.
